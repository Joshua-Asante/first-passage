## §1 — Context: the erratum, and what it was costing

**The error.** A per-trade edge of **0.85R** entered the estate on 2026-08-03 labelled *"measured from the venue-edition exports"* ([inverse-requirements §5](../../lab/analysis/c1/eval_inverse_requirements_2026-08-03/RESULTS.md)) — the venue editions being the **withdrawn, pyramided Striker NAS100→MNQ leg** (0.35 trades/cal-day, correlated adds). No derivation was ever published; `run_slow.py:108` hard-codes it. On 2026-08-04 the referent silently mutated: [slow-archetype L123–124](../../lab/analysis/c1/eval_slow_archetype_2026-08-04/RESULTS.md) re-attributed it to *"N1 … ORB-MNQ-1's realized edge"* — while ORB's realized edge is **+0.0626R** (n=1,846, [re-park ADR](2026-08-03-orb-mnq-repark-payability-falsified.md)) and N1 contains no per-trade R at all. The false binding then propagated: MNQ.md **N13** → EM screen **EM2** ($325 @ 0.85R cell) → **MNQDTL-1** §1.2 (L=$325 elected as "the published MNQ 0.85R EM2 frontier cell"), §1.3 ("0.85R — MNQ best-ever measured (ORB-MNQ-1, N1) … clears (+$26)"), §3.2 O1 ("ORB edge is real: 0.85R per-trade"). [MNQBASE-1 §1.3](../briefs/rnd-pipeline/MNQBASE-1-tradeify-shaped-base-construct-harvest-scoping.md) — authored 2026-08-04, *before* the target spec — already published the correct table and was not consulted. (`lesson_metric_cohort_provenance_binding` + `lesson_borrowed_numbers_need_connecting_arithmetic`, both firing on a ratified surface.)

**The corrected cohort table (canonical; supersedes every "MNQ best-ever measured 0.85R" quotation):**

| Cohort | Per-trade edge | Cadence | Independent entries? | Admissible at the incumbent eval? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Striker NAS100→MNQ (withdrawn) | **0.85R** | 0.35/cal-day | ✗ pyramid = correlated adds | ✗ barred leg; EM3-dead shape |

**What it was costing.** The ratified target (MNQDTL-1: D1 daily cadence + D2 $325 + μ ≥ $130/day = 0.40R at L=$325) demands an intersection — {MNQ × daily × ≥0.40R net/trade independent} — that **no measured construct occupied at authoring time, on the one oracle cell then in evidence** (Q-MNQSEL-1's restart-clock ceiling, S3 < 0.40 both arms). The spec's feasibility row was the phantom. Meanwhile the venue itself binds almost nothing: the [seed-target study](../../lab/analysis/c1/tradeify_seed_target_spec_2026-08-04/RESULTS.md) measured the binding constraint as *"the weekly activity rule and nothing else"*, with admissibility reaching ≈+0.10R at $75/trade under the no-time-limit fact. Four Route B campaigns (2026-08-06→08) returned empty candidate lists under a design whose promotion floor (\|ρ\| = 0.02 at σ(r) ≈ 6.9e-4 ≈ **0.3 pt per 1σ of signal over 60 s**) sits an order of magnitude below the 0.95 pt round trip and the 3–9 pt/trade band EM1 requires — a search that could not have licensed a viable candidate even on success. Left standing, MNQDTL-1's F-B was on track to fire `FALSIFIED` on 2026-11-08 by near-arithmetic, after ~3 more months of pre-determined dry campaigns.

**Superseded by events, in the program's favor, before this ADR even ratifies.** Between drafting and committing this packet, `Q-MNQSEL-2` landed (via `origin/main` PR #693, same day) measuring a *different* oracle cell — dense RTH 1m opens at G=10 pt — clearing 0.40R with real margin (S3 ≈0.858 both arms) and sitting inside the catalogue-K-wall's 5–20 pt viable stop band. `Q-MNQDTL-CON-1` opened the same day to scope a named-entry construct on it, `OPEN`/unpaid. This is exactly the *"different causal candidate set"* Q-MNQSEL-1's own re-proposal bar called for (§3.1 door — not reopened, discharged forward). It does not change §1's provenance finding (the 0.85R cell is still a phantom, ORB's realized edge is still +0.0626R) — it changes the answer to "does a promising non-phantom cell exist" from "not yet measured" to "yes, oracle-side, unconstructed." **This strengthens rather than weakens the case for 2-B**: Q-MNQDTL-CON-1's own §3 frames its eventual GO as scoring against "MNQDTL D1/D2" — the same phantom-anchored gate this ADR retires. Landing the necessity retarget now means that construct is scored against what the venue actually requires, not against an income target with no antecedent in the evidence that motivated it.
