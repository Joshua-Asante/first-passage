## §1 — Context

The concept ADR admits a protection instance to `POLICY_REGISTRY` only by (1) pre-registering an objective and selection rule, (2) running a trigger × scale grid, (3) passing the regime-robustness gate on both halves, (4) freezing the winning row with an admitting ADR. The operator selected the Tradeify portfolio's instance on 2026-09-10 — a 1 % combined-account trigger with 40 % scaling on Aegis / Vanguard / Striker and an ORB response of "base unchanged, adds disabled" — from **unregistered exploratory analysis** (the acceptance record says so in terms), and ruled on 2026-09-11 (D-B4, D-B11) that Track B is a **K = 1 confirmation** of that fixed instance with **no policy grid and no alternative that may win**. A K = 1 confirmation is not step 2. Without a superseding record the admission chain cannot complete for this instance at all, and a `POLICY_REGISTRY` row would be forbidden by the concept ADR §5.

This ADR is that record. It replaces step 2 with the operator's fixed selection, fixes the instance's `reference_mode` instead of dispatching it, records the ORB leg's mode response in place of the multiplier, keeps steps 1, 3 and 4 — step 3 with its three criteria intact, Part B evaluated with a stricter statistic and Part A at a reduced per-panel depth — and, the part earlier revisions lacked, traces the whole lifecycle once (§2a) so that qualification, admission, sealing and deployment authorization are one sequence with one owner per state, on both the success and the failure paths. Every summary in this file, every parser-visible edge and every downstream packet obligation (§2c) is derived from that trace.

## §2 — Decision

1. **The instance.** Registry key `tradeify_portfolio@Tradeify_Select_100K`; `ProtectionPolicy(reference_mode="trailing", trigger=0.01, scale=0.40, provenance="<this ADR> — ratified <date>; selection 2026-09-10; TB-E1 seal <replay-fingerprint digest>")`. `trailing` is the geometry of the selection formula (drawdown against the account's own **running EOD equity peak**); the tier's `dd_type` maps to `locking` in `reference_mode_for_dd_type`, and the evaluation's lock offset is unreachable, so the locking geometry degenerates to trailing for this instance. **The row records `trailing`.** Because the concept ADR §2 item 2 says `reference_mode` is "dispatched from `firm_rules` `dd_type`", and that dispatch would yield `locking`, this ADR supersedes-in-part §2 item 2 for this instance only: the instance's `reference_mode` is fixed by this record, not dispatched, and TB-D0's admission test asserts that no code path resolves this instance through `reference_mode_for_dd_type` (the helper itself is unchanged for every other tier). If a future tier change makes the lock reachable, the instance is re-admitted, never edited.
2. **The mechanism is the concept ADR's invariant, with one recorded per-leg response map.** Trigger compare with six-decimal rounding, inclusive (ULP ADR); mode for weekday D computed once from the prior settled close (no intraday change, no latch, clears when the drawdown falls below the trigger); the scale **multiplies** normal exposure and compounds with the lifecycle haircut before the integer floor; frozen constants untouched. The per-leg response to the fired instance is a property of the book, recorded here because the concept's "single tier, one factor" invariant does not by itself say what one factor means for four legs: Aegis (fixed 8 → 3), Vanguard (quantity-floor → 0, accepted D-B10), Striker (risk-scaled ladder, O-5 law B; adds from executed base, O-6), **ORB: base unchanged at one micro, adds not placed while protected** (the operator's selection, D-B11; a mode response, not a multiplier — the one departure from the concept ADR §2 item 1's "the factor multiplies" invariant, superseded-in-part for this leg of this instance by the fourth header line; the invariant stands for the other three legs and for every other instance). The map lives in `book_policy.BOOK_LEGS` / TB-S1 once TB-I1 corrects it (§6); the registry row carries the three variables only.
3. **Step 2 of the concept ADR §4 is superseded for this instance by the operator's fixed selection.** No trigger × scale grid is run; no alternative cell may be evaluated or promoted (D-B4, D-B11); a failed confirmation ends the attempt with **no admission** and does not reopen the policy choice.
4. **Steps 1, 3 and 4 are retained and mapped onto Track B.**
   - *Step 1 — pre-registration:* TB-P1 drafts and **TB-F1 freezes** the K = 1 confirmation contract (objective = confirm the fixed instance clears the plan's four acceptance conditions at least sizing intervention; selection rule = none, K = 1; the gate below, Part A included) **before** TB-E1 runs. The draft on PR #363 must carry Part A and the deviations below verbatim before TB-F1 (§2c).
   - *Step 3 — the regime-robustness gate, compared limb by limb with its governing procedure:* the gate is **mandatory** here (its scope names "a new `dd_geometry` POLICY_REGISTRY instance"), so its Part C criteria are the admission test, and each deviation from its canonical procedure is stated and ratified, not assumed. **Part C criterion 1 (Part A):** retained as the gate's own statistic — 100 alternate-history panels built from **6-month contiguous blocks** of the replay's venue-session series, each replayed continuously at the fixed instance; the **5th percentile of the per-panel pass-rate distribution must be ≥ the full-panel floor**; the close-call rule (n = 200 when p5 is within 1 pp of the floor) and the sanity checks (p5 ≤ the full-panel pass-rate; H1 + H2 path counts ≈ full) are retained. **Deviation A1 (ratified by this ADR):** the per-panel path depth is a frozen field sized to the compute budget (`OWED-BY: TB-F1`) rather than the parent stream's depth, because every path is a continuous replay (TB-S2 RC-3); this is a reduction in depth, not a removal, and it is the only change to Part A. **Part C criteria 2–3 (Part B):** the halves are the plan's H1 and H2 (chronological midpoint on venue sessions, `OWED-BY: TB-F1`), each replayed independently as the gate requires. **Deviation B1 (stricter, recorded so the substitution is visible):** the contract's limb on each half is a one-sided 95 % upper bound on the failure proportion ≤ 0.05, which implies the gate's point criterion (pass-rate ≥ 0.95) and never passes a half the gate would fail; the gate's criterion is therefore satisfied a fortiori, not replaced. **Floor:** the gate requires its floor to equal the brief's full-panel floor; the contract's full-panel limb is the same 5 % failure ceiling, so the floor is 0.95 on point pass-rates for Part A and the 5 % bound on every limb of Part B — no separate regime floor exists. **Streams:** Part A and Part B run **once, inside TB-E1, on the n2 stream**; their results enter the replay fingerprint and are the retained admission evidence. **TB-E2 evaluates exactly the plan's four n3 conditions** (F-full, F-H1, F-H2, S) on the fresh n3 stream — F-H1/F-H2 are Part B evaluated again on n3 by construction of the plan, Part A is **not rerun** (no defined stream or budget exists for it and D-B4 forbids an extra sample).
   - *Step 4 — freeze, admitting ADR and the two-stage admission:* as §2a stages T3–T11. In one sentence: TB-E1's seal freezes the row's provenance; **TB-D0 = executable admission** (the row lands after TB-E1, before the Phase 8 live test and B7, so the executable tested, sealed and used by n3 already carries the final row — D-B11); **TB-E2 = deployment qualification** (the sole n3); **TB-D2 + the GO reseal = deployment authorization** (a baked-in `DEPLOYMENT_GO.json` on an artifact-only commit, §2b); the arm is refused at every stage before T11 by a host-visible check.
5. **Evidence class stated honestly.** The selection evidence (`dd-orb-base-only-2026-09-10`, private digests in the acceptance record) is unregistered exploratory analysis and is cited as provenance only; it is not confirmation evidence and no figure from it is quoted. Confirmation evidence is TB-E1 (screen, n1, n2, Part A) and TB-E2 (sole n3) on the frozen contract, numbers private per D-B12.

**Effective:** on the operator's dated ratification addendum. Until then this ADR authorizes nothing and `POLICY_REGISTRY` stays empty.

### §2b — The deployment-GO artifact and the GO reseal (definition)

- **Shape and placement.** `docs/notes/rail_build/DEPLOYMENT_GO.json`, baked into the listener image as `M1_MONITORING_ACCEPTANCE.json` is (`.dockerignore` re-include, Dockerfile `COPY`), never read from the volume — the arm CLI's own rationale (§0) forbids gating on a mutable file — with one difference the M1 pattern does not need: the `COPY` must **tolerate the path's absence** (an optional-source form — a wildcard source such as `DEPLOYMENT_GO.jso[n]` listed beside a source that always exists — because a named `COPY` of an absent path fails the build, `.dockerignore` lines 78–80), so that image v1 builds at T5 with no artifact and the arm refuses on absence, the fail-safe direction the Dockerfile already documents for M1. No placeholder artifact is baked: absence is the fail-closed state, and R5 and the §10 hook stay mechanical. Fields as T10. Written **only** by TB-D2, after the operator's recorded deployment GO, in a commit that changes **no other path**.
- **Validator and interlock (built before B7, offline).** `ops/c1_rail/deployment_go.py` (schema + `validate(path)` + the digest comparisons of T11) and the `c1_rail_arm.py --arm` check, both owned by **TB-I3** with tests: refused on a missing, malformed, non-GO, expired or mismatched artifact, on a row/FBR mismatch, and without the T11 no-activity attestation (an explicit `--arm` input recorded in the arm evidence; attested by the operator's host read, not machine-verified — no account read API exists, seal contract §Output). TB-I3 also verifies the v1 build with the path absent and a fixture v2 build with it present. Image v1 therefore already refuses the arm; no repository-side marker is needed in the T4 → T10 window.
- **Why the reseal preserves the qualification.** n3 is a replay on FBR started from S; its result is bound to the FBR digest and the S digest (seal contract), not to an image hash. The artifact-only commit changes no shared component, so FBR equality — the qualification — is unchanged; the reseal repeats the B7 shared-component proof mechanically, compares the v1/v2 image manifests and requires every layer except the added GO layer to have the identical digest in the identical order, and reuses S within its validity. A mutable base tag, cache miss or other rebuild that changes any non-GO layer therefore refuses the reseal rather than silently qualifying a new runtime. Anything beyond the artifact-only build-context diff is not a reseal: a shared-component change ends the attempt (there is no second n3), a non-shared build-context change forces a fresh B7 and, through the seal contract's binding, a TB-E2 rerun from the fresh seal (R1c) — the reading of "no extra sample" applied here is **no additional or combined sample; a replacement run after a void seal is the seal contract's own rule**, and the operator confirms this reading at ratification.
- **Failure path.** GO is never written on R1a/R1b; on R1b the row-removal commit is redeployed before any later arm, and both images refuse the arm for independent reasons (T8).
