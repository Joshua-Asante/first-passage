## Decision

Name "explore" as charter step **5a** (between step 5 G0 freeze and step 6 Pine) — non-disruptive insertion, not a renumber (steps 6–8 keep their numbers; existing external step-number citations are unaffected). Gate criteria — landed in the charter's step 5a in this same commit, not invented here — are codified from 4/4 observed exercises (C2/C1/S2A/C3-K2): entry gated by an operator-issued `EXPLORE_GO.md` token (post-G0 only, never self-issued); IS-panel-only scoring (sha-verified, CONFIRM stays unread) via session-block bootstrap CI plus Req 1a DELETE/FLIP; verdict routes per the card's own `PREREG_G0.md` — `FALSIFIED` / `SHAPE-CLEAR` (only route to step 6) / `AMBIGUOUS-HOLD` / `VOID`. Full route table lives at charter step 5a (Reads, below) — not restated here to stay under the light-tier word cap.
