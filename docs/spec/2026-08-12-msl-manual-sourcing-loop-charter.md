# SPEC: MSL — manual sourcing loop (operator composition channel)

Status: RATIFIED 2026-08-12 · authorizes nothing ($0 · K=0) · depends: [TNEC-1](2026-08-08-tradeify-necessary-conditions-target-spec.md) (intake gate) · [edge-cohort ADR §2-C](../adr/2026-08-08-edge-cohort-correction-and-necessity-retarget.md) (sourcing law L2/L3; MSL added in part via ratification ADR) · [S6](2026-08-07-loop-s6-k-aware-generation-spec.md) · [EM0–EM5](2026-08-05-eval-mechanism-shape-screen.md) · [TNEC-AU-1](2026-08-11-tnec-application-unit-book-admission.md) (PROPOSED — see step 8 interaction rule)
Objective: Establish the FXIFY-era operator+Claude composition loop (idea → $0 screens → Pine → operator TV backtest → survivor MC) as a **named sourcing channel** feeding TNEC-1 intake — generation modality changes; no gate weakens.

Three operator rulings (ratification ADR [`2026-08-12-msl-sourcing-channel-ratification.md`](../adr/2026-08-12-msl-sourcing-channel-ratification.md) — **Accepted** 2026-08-12; elections identical here):
1. **R-CHANNEL** — ☑ accepted: ADR 2026-08-08 §2-C L2 gains **MSL** as a named channel (in-part addition). The MNQBASE-1 bar's operative sentence is quoted in full in the ADR — *"a new sourcing channel (Req 1b-grade published cohort, or a constraint-based WHO not yet enumerated) — not another pass over the same classes… New mechanism evidence does [clear it]"* — MSL claims neither prong by assertion; for MNQ-scoped work the bar is cleared per-candidate only under R-REQSCOPE. MNQBASE-1 stays closed (P1–P6 pre-filters, L2 STOP); MNQDTL-1's §3.1 doors C1–C11 stand separately.
2. **R-FRAMING** — ☑ §2.1 governs (non-index cards proceed; framing recorded per card in the door-check). Fade-spec §7 adverse-reading-wins is not the governing precedent for MSL. Until this election, non-index cards stopped before G0 — that stop is discharged.
3. **R-REQSCOPE** — ☑ they do not bind composition: harvest Req 1b/Req 2 do **not** bind internally-composed candidates. MSL candidates enter under the estate's G0/explore-confirm lane discipline (dense-1m precedent), with **Req 1a (constraint-selects-the-trade, delete/flip) still binding** as the mechanism-admission test and EM0–EM5 + TNEC-1 unchanged.

Steps (the per-idea checklist — every candidate walks 1→8 in order; two kill classes: **pre-G0 kill** = a step 2–4 FAIL, records a pre-screen rejection; **scored kill** = a step 8 FAIL, records a registry entry + verdict string):
1. **Slate card** exists first: idea + mechanism story (who loses money and why), **one** construct class named in the card by mechanism reasoning with zero data contact (a class menu is K_intrinsic per member), instrument pinned by mechanism-independent reasoning — never by comparing scores across instruments; a second instrument after scoring is a new K spend.
2. **Dedup + door-check, executed not asserted**: paste `rg` output over `docs/rejected_candidates.md` + `lab/CATALOG.md` + briefs bar-rulings (search by mechanism family AND role); declare the mechanism id (nearest class or NEW) in `ops/instruments/MECHANISMS.md` **at the card commit** (the growth rule's "pre-registration" includes the slate card), listing rejected nearest classes with one-line reasons; run `scripts/instrument_profiles.py cell <SYMBOL> <mechanism-id>` (tier=always) and answer **every** BINDING BAR by route in writing. ⚠ Non-index ledgers may carry **no** `bars:` section (verified: MGC emits zero bars) — domain bars the tool cannot surface (SNAG bar, R-FRAMING status) are answered as **explicit card line items that block G0 independent of tool output**; registering missing domain bars in the instrument ledger is that card's Stage-0 task.
3. **$0 arithmetic screens** — three evaluated at **one declared design point (k, contracts, entries/day)**; any FAIL is a pre-G0 kill:
   – cost-law: gross/trade ≥ 4× all-in RT (MYM/MNQ/M2K: RT = 2×$0.91 + 2×$0.50-tick slippage = **$2.82** ⇒ ≥ **$11.28**/contract/trade; re-price per instrument from its own tick/commission schedule);
   – payability: an all-win day at the design point clears **$200**;
   – survival: worst *achievable* EOD day at the design point ≤ **$750**.
   Disclosures (stated, non-gating): **implied annualized SR** = q·√(252·n), where q = m/σ of the two-point law (m = p·rr − (1−p); σ = (rr+1)·√(p(1−p)); n = designed trades/day) — Aegis **1.83** / futures-native **≈0.89** are cohort disclosures, **not a FAIL** ([`IMPLIED-SR-REPORT-ONLY-2026-08-13`](../adr/2026-08-13-implied-sr-report-only-fade-reopen.md); the 2026-08-10 freeze-time kill is superseded); σ_d at the design point vs the $3,000 trail · best-day-≤-40%-of-total shape · the **0.40R/trade inversion line** (arithmetic only, never admissibility — the slow-archetype $325@0.85R cell is **void as provenance**, phantom pyramided cohort, TNEC-1 N-SIZE). No pass-speed is promised. Measured-edge results continue to gate on DSR-at-K.
   **Data-read rule:** any pre-G0 measurement (delete/flip scoring, entry-rate counts) runs only on a declared IS partition; the CONFIRM window is named in the card **before** any step-3 read and stays unread through step 8.
4. **Cheap falsifier before authoring** where one exists (<~5 min, parent-side, designed GENEROUS so failure is conclusive).
5. **G0 pre-registration freeze** — executed by the campaign manager **only on the operator's B4 GO** (no self-freeze): K_intrinsic per axis declared; CONFIRM holdout re-reserved; **sweep axes pre-registered as robustness probes** (plateau reads) — selection is in-sample only.
5a. **Explore confirm** ([2026-08-14](../adr/2026-08-14-msl-explore-stage-5a.md), non-disruptive insertion — steps 6–8 keep their numbers) — gated by an operator/campaign-manager-issued `EXPLORE_GO.md` token (gitignored, issued only after the G0 freeze, never self-issued); scores the frozen construct on the pinned IS panel only (sha-verified; CONFIRM stays reserved and unread) via session-block bootstrap 95% CI per pre-registered arm plus Req 1a DELETE/FLIP; verdict routes per the card's `PREREG_G0.md`: `FALSIFIED` (all arms CI-upper < 0, or a card-specific measured-rate trigger e.g. N-ACT < 1 trade/week) · `SHAPE-CLEAR` (≥1 arm CI-lo > 0 ∧ DELETE PASS ∧ FLIP PASS ∧ aux live-pass — the only route that proceeds to step 6) · `AMBIGUOUS-HOLD` (otherwise) · `VOID` (panel/token/coverage defect); a DELETE/FLIP PASS never rescues a CI-FALSIFIED primary.
6. **Pine authored CC-solo** (surface-allocation test 1: Pine never fleets); `pine_lint` + operator TV paste is the compile gate (lint passes code TV rejects — CE10237 precedent); runbook names exact inputs, date window, chart TZ.
7. **Operator TV backtest** (S2: TV login automation stays prohibited) — the runbook **must carry links to the step 2–5 artifacts** (dedup block · door-check record · screens table · G0 PR#) and **the operator refuses the TV seat if any is missing** (this is the FALSIFIED-condition chokepoint). Export → local copy; **static-equity recompute before any param compare** (TV compounds).
8. **Survivor MC is the verdict, not TV net/PF**: corrected geometry is the `firm_rules` **default** since 2026-08-04 (`dd_lock_offset_usd=1_000_000.0` — verify at read, never re-patch); intraday-honest limb only where the daily series carries a true intraday low — a series reconstructed from trade closes is labeled **LOWER BOUND** unless TV per-trade Run-up/Drawdown columns bound within-trade excursion; emit the TNEC verdict string `N-ACT N-SURV N-EDGE N-SHAPE N-SIZE | bust | P(pass) | μ(disclosed)`. Kill → registry entry (interaction rule: single-construct verdicts govern; if TNEC-AU-1 ratifies, a standalone-cadence-only kill routes to the book-candidate pool per AU-1 instead). Survive → TNEC-1 intake → operator GO chain (M1 + per-session GO untouched).

Gate: RESOLVED when the first card reaches a recorded outcome with its step-artifacts on record — pre-G0 kill, scored kill, or survive all count (cheap honest kills are the win, per S6). FALSIFIED (process) if any card reaches step 7 without the step 2–5 artifacts, any step executes out of order, or a sweep result is used for selection without a pre-registered axis. FALSIFIED (yield) if **6 consecutive cards die pre-G0 across ≥2 instrument families**, or **12 calendar weeks pass with zero G0 freezes** — the channel closes pending a superseding ADR (the plan's 3-death Board review is the earlier rung of this ladder, not a substitute). FALSIFIED (yield-conversion) — added [2026-08-14](../adr/2026-08-14-msl-yield-falsifier-survival-limb.md), two-rung, addition only (does not alter the yield/process clauses above): **WATCH** (soft, non-terminal) if **6 consecutive G0 freezes** occur with **zero cards reaching step 6 (Pine authored)** — fires a mandatory Board review of the explore-stage screen calibration, does not itself close the channel. **FALSIFIED** (hard, terminal, same consequence as FALSIFIED (yield)) fires on whichever comes first: **10 consecutive G0 freezes** with **zero TNEC-1 survivors**, or **8 calendar weeks** from MSL's first G0 freeze (2026-08-12) with **zero TNEC-1 survivors** given **≥4 G0 freezes** in that window — the channel closes pending a superseding ADR.
