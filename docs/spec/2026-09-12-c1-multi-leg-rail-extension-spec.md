**Action classes.** *Risk-adding:* `entry`, `add`, a **loosening** `amend` (a stop moved away from the market; for a trail, a larger activation distance or a larger offset), a **cancel of a protective order outside `CLOSE`/`AMEND`** while its position is not `CONFIRMED(0)`, a cancel whose target kind is unknown. *Risk-reducing:* `exit`, `flat`, `closeposition`, a cancel of a resting `entry`/`add`, a **tightening** `amend`, an `attach` of the port's first protection to a bare lot, a cancel of a protective order whose position is `CONFIRMED(0)` (orphan removal — a surviving stop could open the other side). The listener classifies every action from `pending[order_ref].kind`, **fresh** `W` and the leg's confirmed allocation, never from the daemon's label; tightening versus loosening is decided against the working order's current level in `W`, so an `amend` whose target is not in a fresh `W` cannot be classified and is refused (S3).

**Evidence currency (applies to every primitive).** An operation's completion, and any no-op determination, uses only `P`/`W` evidence whose `as_of` is **strictly after** the operation's `prepared_at` — an equal instant proves no read-after-prepare order; the one-bar freshness window is an upper bound on age, never a substitute for postdating. A 16:25 snapshot cannot complete a 16:30 close, and neither can a read stamped 16:30. Gap detection runs on order-level reads only: a position-only read never manufactures a gap. A **position-only read** (no working-order list, no order status) never completes or settles an operation, exactly as it never resolves an order (`pending` row): absent order-level data is not flatness.

**Protection identity and allocation (K2; applies to every per-fill shorthand below).** An immutable protection owner is created from the originating entry fill; its bracket, quantity, consumed flag and trailing activation/anchor are separate from the FIFO lots that an exit execution reduces. Evidence identifies both the triggering order/protection owner and the allocated entry fills. The qualified emulator's `_fill_exit` consumes the triggering bracket, allocates the exit FIFO and retains untriggered sibling orders even if their original lot is exhausted; `_amend` updates those surviving owners and never recreates a consumed bracket. An exhausted original lot is therefore not sufficient proof of a protective orphan, a consumed protection operation or a protection gap. Validate the surviving protection set and its executable coverage of the residual position. Full-symbol quiescence still requires all owners/orders removed. Explicit scoped market closes use the distinct `_close_scope` allocation/removal transition; never infer its result from a triggered-bracket fill or blindly transfer/cancel an owner. L2 qualification must prove both transition types preserve I7 and the pinned emulator lifecycle; if they cannot both be met, that transition is `BLOCKED — capability-problem` pending owner adjudication, not an allowed semantic substitution. No emulator or port behavior is changed by this specification.

**Execution primitives (defined once; every sequence that touches protection uses them).**
- **`CLOSE(scope)`** — persist the transition kind as well as scope: `explicit_scope` (one fill, leg or symbol) or `triggered_protection` (S3(d), identified by the protective owner with FIFO allocation). Different transition kinds are different demands and cannot reuse one operation identity. All shared ownership/retry/evidence rules apply; the route must qualify the selected transition, not substitute one for the other. Requires verified `L2(d)` and `L2(e)`: a broker-native scoped reduction that atomically adjusts the scope's attached protection to the remaining quantity on every partial fill and removes it on full-scope quiescence, using the distinct protection-owner/FIFO-allocation mapping above; the close and protection cannot over-execute into a reverse position. The listener confirms completion from `P` and `W` evidence that postdates `prepared_at`, never from HTTP acceptance. For `CLOSE(leg)` / `CLOSE(symbol)` with unknown quantity, use the verified quantity-less position-close primitive on the exclusively owned symbol (D-B9), never a guessed quantity or a fabricated fill scope. **No sequential or concurrent cancel-plus-market-close fallback is built:** putting two requests in one software step does not make broker execution atomic. Persist the operation identity, scope, `prepared_at` and outcome before send. **Overlapping operations on one symbol queue in turn:** one close in flight per symbol; a later, broader close (EOD, kill, recovery) on a symbol with an unresolved narrower close is `queued` and dispatched after the earlier broker attempt is reconciled and nonpending — never dropped. Serialization concerns broker attempts, not discharge of every completion obligation: once a bounded quantity has executed, queued full recovery may proceed while the bounded operation still owns invalid residual protection; that bounded quantity must not be resent; only an identical demand may reuse an existing operation; a stronger full-scope demand following a bounded reduction retains separate queued ownership, even on the same scope. Set the account-wide `unknown_order` block while a close is unresolved; clear it only after evidence resolves every operation owning that reason. Rejection retains the existing protection and sets the durable **`close_rejected`** block for a scope that still has exposure (owner: the listener's retry — after reconciliation on postdating evidence the same operation is resubmitted at most once per bar, alerting each time; each rejection owner clears when its own requested reduction completes on covering evidence: full closes require quiescence; bounded closes require the requested quantity executed, confirmed cancellation of relevant remainders and valid protection on the entire residual. The aggregate reason remains while any rejection owner remains. A recorded operator clear after attended recovery resolves only its named owner); an unknown outcome is reconciled from postdating evidence before resubmission, never blindly retried; a **partial** outcome (residual exposure on postdating evidence) resumes through the **same** operation, for the remainder only, after that reconciliation — never a new operation. Recovery resumes the same durable operation after restart and alerts on unresolved exposure. If the route cannot meet these properties, new exposure is blocked at admission and the live packet is `BLOCKED — capability-problem`; a capability failure discovered with exposure already present retains protection, blocks risk-adds and requires attended recovery rather than inventing unsupported commands. A `CLOSE` whose scope is already `CONFIRMED(0)` with no attached orders **on evidence that postdates `prepared_at`** completes as a no-op without a send (idempotent by scope), subject to the §2e gross-lot, working-remainder and request-fence rules, so an adapter's own `flat` arriving after a scheduler, kill or recovery `CLOSE` never produces a second order. *[PROPOSED 2026-10-02, not adopted: for a whole-leg C-a close, [§1.1a](#11a-replaced-or-qualified-clauses) C1–C6 qualify the L2(d)/(e) requirement and replace the rejection resubmission, the unknown-outcome resubmission, the remainder resumption and the restart resumption with the incident outcomes O5–O7 (CL 7.3, 7.6.2, 7.6.3).]*
- **`AMEND(order_ref, level | qty)`** — change of protection the port has already defined (recomputed stop, trailing parameters, bracket quantity). Requires verified `L2(c)`: broker-native atomic modify, with the old protection remaining effective until the new protection takes effect and with no interval where two independent stops can over-execute. **Requires the target in a fresh `W`** (`as_of` within one bar and after the last operation on that fill): with `W = UNKNOWN` the amend is refused (`amend_deferred`), `W` is reconciled through R-M, and the port's next per-bar re-issue supplies the level again — nothing is guessed, and a loosening change can never pass as tightening. **Cancel/replace is not a supported realization.** The listener diffs the re-issued bracket against `expected_protection[fill]` and modifies **only the components whose level or parameters changed**; unchanged components — in particular an active native trail — are never re-sent, so the broker's accumulated trailing anchor is never disturbed (the emulator likewise keeps `trail_active` / `trail_extreme` when it replaces a bracket). Persist the operation identity and target before send; an unresolved operation on the same lot defers a new amend (`amend_deferred`) until it resolves. Rejection leaves the old protection working; an unknown outcome sets the account-wide `unknown_order` block and reconciles `P` and `W` (postdating evidence) before another modification or resubmission. If reconciliation proves an expected protective order absent, set the account-wide `protection_gap` block and initiate the verified `CLOSE(scope)` recovery; retain the block and alert until broker evidence confirms flatness and removal of the scope's orders. Recovery is not a substitute for atomic modify capability. A tightening `AMEND` is risk-reducing; a loosening one is risk-adding.
- **`ATTACH(fill scope, bracket)`** — the port's **first** protection for a lot that entered bare (the emulator's "attach an order to a lot that never had one": Striker issues its exit a bar after the entry). Requires verified `L2(f)`: the route can attach stop/limit/trailing protection to an existing open fill **linked to the position** so that `L2(d)`/`L2(e)` semantics hold afterwards — a standalone stop that would survive a close is not an attach. Persisted like `AMEND`; a **rejection is a `protection_gap` immediately** — the exposure is known unprotected — with the recovery `CLOSE(scope)` started at once; an unknown outcome is reconciled from postdating evidence and becomes a gap if the protection is not working. An unknown amend or attach is never resubmitted through the close retry path; the port's next re-issue supplies it. The listener chooses `ATTACH` versus `AMEND` from `expected_protection[fill]` (empty → attach), not from the daemon's label, because the port emits one `BracketAmend` shape for both.

**Invariants.** **I1** — an `UNKNOWN` in any state an action reads refuses every risk-adding action. Risk-reducing requests remain admissible; broker dispatch still obeys the operation-identity, scope, evidence-currency and reconciliation rules of the primitives, so admission never means guessing a target or duplicating an unresolved send. **I2** — only broker evidence moves `P`, `W`, `pending` past `accepted`, `ledger.confirmed`, the *working* status inside `expected_protection` and any adapter position state; intent moves `pending` to `sent`, takes a reservation and records the **intended** protection, nothing else. **I3** — every obligation survives restart and clears only by its own defined completion or explicit owner-specific operator clear. A next-session transition never clears trading permission; only rev8 operator resume can do so after all required reconciliation. **I4** — exactly one authority computes a broker quantity for a risk-adding order: the listener's sizing host (R-Q); `OrderIntent.qty` on `entry`/`add` is the **adapter-normal** quantity (a positive int, as the delivered constructor requires) and reaches the listener as `qty_normal`; a broker quantity travels only on `exit`/`flat`. For S3(d) close-time exits, `order_ref` names the triggering protection owner; for explicit exits it is absent and `scope_refs` names accounting fills. R-B1/R-B3 validate these mutually exclusive shapes and refuse an unknown owner or ambiguous shape before dispatch. **I5** — a checkpoint and its execution cursor are one atomic write; an event id is applied at most once. **I6** — the listener's `blocks` refuse every risk-adding admission while risk-reducing actions remain subject to their primitive preconditions; daemon quiescence is a second layer, never the safety property. **I7 (rev 5.3)** — every admitted protection transition preserves **the protection the strategy has defined** for the remaining position continuously, including between broker events, and cannot leave an executable orphan that opens reverse exposure: `AMEND` changes protection atomically, `ATTACH` adds it atomically, `CLOSE` reduces position and protection together *[PROPOSED 2026-10-02, not adopted: qualified for C-a by [§1.1a](#11a-replaced-or-qualified-clauses) C7]*. A lot the port has not yet protected (a bare Striker base before its first amend) is qualified behaviour, not a gap; from the moment the port defines protection, its absence from fresh `W` is a `protection_gap` — an incident requiring the durable recovery above, not a permitted transient state or a promise that recovery always succeeds. The rail never adds protection the port did not define. **I8 (rev 5.3)** — the rail executes only the semantics the emulator qualified: (i) resting stop entries filled intrabar; (ii) protection per lot exactly as the port defines it — attached at entry when the intent carries a bracket, otherwise attached by the port's first amend, re-issued per bar and taking effect from the next bar's path, **except** that for `orders_on_close` legs a newly issued fixed stop/limit already crossed by the generating close fills at that close (`_evaluate_at_close`), which the rail realizes as a market `exit` with the triggering protection identity and FIFO lifecycle of S3(d) at the signal — the emulator's own rule evaluated on the bar the daemon already holds, not a new exit rule; (iii) trailing exactly as the port's `Bracket` parameters define it, realized by a native trailing stop (activation at fill ± activation ticks, then the traded extreme ∓ offset, effective stop the tighter of fixed and trailing); (iv) market orders at the signal. Two deviation classes are accepted, both measured in Phase 8 execution parity against frozen tolerances (`OWED-BY: TB-F1`): **latency** (an order reaches the broker seconds after the bar close instead of at `THIS_CLOSE`/`NEXT_OPEN`) and the **fill-path** class (the emulator's intrabar path assumption versus actual ticks for stops and trails — the TV-versus-live gap already inherent to the parity evidence). A semantic substitute — a daemon-held stop, a daemon-evaluated intrabar stop, a synthesized trail, protection on a lot the port left bare — is a different strategy and is never built. The separately ratified S6 emergency flats are operational risk-reduction exceptions, not claims of replay parity; all incident recovery is listener-owned under rev8; §5 authorizes the control/report amendment.
