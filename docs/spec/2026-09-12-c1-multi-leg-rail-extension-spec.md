**Action classes.** *Risk-adding:* `entry`, `add`, a **loosening** `amend` (a stop moved away from the market; for a trail, a larger activation distance or a larger offset), a **cancel of a protective order outside `CLOSE`/`AMEND`** while its position is not `CONFIRMED(0)`, a cancel whose target kind is unknown. *Risk-reducing:* `exit`, `flat`, `closeposition`, a cancel of a resting `entry`/`add`, a **tightening** `amend`, an `attach` of the port's first protection to a bare lot, a cancel of a protective order whose position is `CONFIRMED(0)` (orphan removal — a surviving stop could open the other side). The listener classifies every action from `pending[order_ref].kind`, **fresh** `W` and the leg's confirmed allocation, never from the daemon's label; tightening versus loosening is decided against the working order's current level in `W`, so an `amend` whose target is not in a fresh `W` cannot be classified and is refused (S3).

**Evidence currency (applies to every primitive).** An operation's completion, and any no-op determination, uses only `P`/`W` evidence whose `as_of` is **strictly after** the operation's `prepared_at` — an equal instant proves no read-after-prepare order; the one-bar freshness window is an upper bound on age, never a substitute for postdating. A 16:25 snapshot cannot complete a 16:30 close, and neither can a read stamped 16:30. Gap detection runs on order-level reads only: a position-only read never manufactures a gap. A **position-only read** (no working-order list, no order status) never completes or settles an operation, exactly as it never resolves an order (`pending` row): absent order-level data is not flatness.

**Protection identity and allocation (K2; applies to every per-fill shorthand below).** An immutable protection owner is created from the originating entry fill; its bracket, quantity, consumed flag and trailing activation/anchor are separate from the FIFO lots that an exit execution reduces. Evidence identifies both the triggering order/protection owner and the allocated entry fills. The qualified emulator's `_fill_exit` consumes the triggering bracket, allocates the exit FIFO and retains untriggered sibling orders even if their original lot is exhausted; `_amend` updates those surviving owners and never recreates a consumed bracket. An exhausted original lot is therefore not sufficient proof of a protective orphan, a consumed protection operation or a protection gap. Validate the surviving protection set and its executable coverage of the residual position. Full-symbol quiescence still requires all owners/orders removed. Explicit scoped market closes use the distinct `_close_scope` allocation/removal transition; never infer its result from a triggered-bracket fill or blindly transfer/cancel an owner. L2 qualification must prove both transition types preserve I7 and the pinned emulator lifecycle; if they cannot both be met, that transition is `BLOCKED — capability-problem` pending owner adjudication, not an allowed semantic substitution. No emulator or port behavior is changed by this specification.

**Execution primitives (defined once; every sequence that touches protection uses them).**
- **`CLOSE(scope)`** — persist the transition kind as well as scope: `explicit_scope` (one fill, leg or symbol) or `triggered_protection` (S3(d), identified by the protective owner with FIFO allocation). Different transition kinds are different demands and cannot reuse one operation identity. All shared ownership/retry/evidence rules apply; the route must qualify the selected transition, not substitute one for the other. Requires verified `L2(d)` and `L2(e)`: a broker-native scoped reduction that atomically adjusts the scope's attached protection to the remaining quantity on every partial fill and removes it on full-scope quiescence, using the distinct protection-owner/FIFO-allocation mapping above; the close and protection cannot over-execute into a reverse position. The listener confirms completion from `P` and `W` evidence that postdates `prepared_at`, never from HTTP acceptance. For `CLOSE(leg)` / `CLOSE(symbol)` with unknown quantity, use the verified quantity-less position-close primitive on the exclusively owned symbol (D-B9), never a guessed quantity or a fabricated fill scope. **No sequential or concurrent cancel-plus-market-close fallback is built:** putting two requests in one software step does not make broker execution atomic. Persist the operation identity, scope, `prepared_at` and outcome before send. **Overlapping operations on one symbol queue in turn:** one close in flight per symbol; a later, broader close (EOD, kill, recovery) on a symbol with an unresolved narrower close is `queued` and dispatched after the earlier broker attempt is reconciled and nonpending — never dropped. Serialization concerns broker attempts, not discharge of every completion obligation: once a bounded quantity has executed, queued full recovery may proceed while the bounded operation still owns invalid residual protection; that bounded quantity must not be resent; only an identical demand may reuse an existing operation; a stronger full-scope demand following a bounded reduction retains separate queued ownership, even on the same scope. Set the account-wide `unknown_order` block while a close is unresolved; clear it only after evidence resolves every operation owning that reason. Rejection retains the existing protection and sets the durable **`close_rejected`** block for a scope that still has exposure (owner: the listener's retry — after reconciliation on postdating evidence the same operation is resubmitted at most once per bar, alerting each time; each rejection owner clears when its own requested reduction completes on covering evidence: full closes require quiescence; bounded closes require the requested quantity executed, confirmed cancellation of relevant remainders and valid protection on the entire residual. The aggregate reason remains while any rejection owner remains. A recorded operator clear after attended recovery resolves only its named owner); an unknown outcome is reconciled from postdating evidence before resubmission, never blindly retried; a **partial** outcome (residual exposure on postdating evidence) resumes through the **same** operation, for the remainder only, after that reconciliation — never a new operation. Recovery resumes the same durable operation after restart and alerts on unresolved exposure. If the route cannot meet these properties, new exposure is blocked at admission and the live packet is `BLOCKED — capability-problem`; a capability failure discovered with exposure already present retains protection, blocks risk-adds and requires attended recovery rather than inventing unsupported commands. A `CLOSE` whose scope is already `CONFIRMED(0)` with no attached orders **on evidence that postdates `prepared_at`** completes as a no-op without a send (idempotent by scope), subject to the §2e gross-lot, working-remainder and request-fence rules, so an adapter's own `flat` arriving after a scheduler, kill or recovery `CLOSE` never produces a second order.
- **`AMEND(order_ref, level | qty)`** — change of protection the port has already defined (recomputed stop, trailing parameters, bracket quantity). Requires verified `L2(c)`: broker-native atomic modify, with the old protection remaining effective until the new protection takes effect and with no interval where two independent stops can over-execute. **Requires the target in a fresh `W`** (`as_of` within one bar and after the last operation on that fill): with `W = UNKNOWN` the amend is refused (`amend_deferred`), `W` is reconciled through R-M, and the port's next per-bar re-issue supplies the level again — nothing is guessed, and a loosening change can never pass as tightening. **Cancel/replace is not a supported realization.** The listener diffs the re-issued bracket against `expected_protection[fill]` and modifies **only the components whose level or parameters changed**; unchanged components — in particular an active native trail — are never re-sent, so the broker's accumulated trailing anchor is never disturbed (the emulator likewise keeps `trail_active` / `trail_extreme` when it replaces a bracket). Persist the operation identity and target before send; an unresolved operation on the same lot defers a new amend (`amend_deferred`) until it resolves. Rejection leaves the old protection working; an unknown outcome sets the account-wide `unknown_order` block and reconciles `P` and `W` (postdating evidence) before another modification or resubmission. If reconciliation proves an expected protective order absent, set the account-wide `protection_gap` block and initiate the verified `CLOSE(scope)` recovery; retain the block and alert until broker evidence confirms flatness and removal of the scope's orders. Recovery is not a substitute for atomic modify capability. A tightening `AMEND` is risk-reducing; a loosening one is risk-adding.
- **`ATTACH(fill scope, bracket)`** — the port's **first** protection for a lot that entered bare (the emulator's "attach an order to a lot that never had one": Striker issues its exit a bar after the entry). Requires verified `L2(f)`: the route can attach stop/limit/trailing protection to an existing open fill **linked to the position** so that `L2(d)`/`L2(e)` semantics hold afterwards — a standalone stop that would survive a close is not an attach. Persisted like `AMEND`; a **rejection is a `protection_gap` immediately** — the exposure is known unprotected — with the recovery `CLOSE(scope)` started at once; an unknown outcome is reconciled from postdating evidence and becomes a gap if the protection is not working. An unknown amend or attach is never resubmitted through the close retry path; the port's next re-issue supplies it. The listener chooses `ATTACH` versus `AMEND` from `expected_protection[fill]` (empty → attach), not from the daemon's label, because the port emits one `BracketAmend` shape for both.

**Invariants.** **I1** — an `UNKNOWN` in any state an action reads refuses every risk-adding action. Risk-reducing requests remain admissible; broker dispatch still obeys the operation-identity, scope, evidence-currency and reconciliation rules of the primitives, so admission never means guessing a target or duplicating an unresolved send. **I2** — only broker evidence moves `P`, `W`, `pending` past `accepted`, `ledger.confirmed`, the *working* status inside `expected_protection` and any adapter position state; intent moves `pending` to `sent`, takes a reservation and records the **intended** protection, nothing else. **I3** — every obligation survives restart and clears only by its own defined completion or explicit owner-specific operator clear. A next-session transition never clears trading permission; only rev8 operator resume can do so after all required reconciliation. **I4** — exactly one authority computes a broker quantity for a risk-adding order: the listener's sizing host (R-Q); `OrderIntent.qty` on `entry`/`add` is the **adapter-normal** quantity (a positive int, as the delivered constructor requires) and reaches the listener as `qty_normal`; a broker quantity travels only on `exit`/`flat`. For S3(d) close-time exits, `order_ref` names the triggering protection owner; for explicit exits it is absent and `scope_refs` names accounting fills. R-B1/R-B3 validate these mutually exclusive shapes and refuse an unknown owner or ambiguous shape before dispatch. **I5** — a checkpoint and its execution cursor are one atomic write; an event id is applied at most once. **I6** — the listener's `blocks` refuse every risk-adding admission while risk-reducing actions remain subject to their primitive preconditions; daemon quiescence is a second layer, never the safety property. **I7 (rev 5.3)** — every admitted protection transition preserves **the protection the strategy has defined** for the remaining position continuously, including between broker events, and cannot leave an executable orphan that opens reverse exposure: `AMEND` changes protection atomically, `ATTACH` adds it atomically, `CLOSE` reduces position and protection together. A lot the port has not yet protected (a bare Striker base before its first amend) is qualified behaviour, not a gap; from the moment the port defines protection, its absence from fresh `W` is a `protection_gap` — an incident requiring the durable recovery above, not a permitted transient state or a promise that recovery always succeeds. The rail never adds protection the port did not define. **I8 (rev 5.3)** — the rail executes only the semantics the emulator qualified: (i) resting stop entries filled intrabar; (ii) protection per lot exactly as the port defines it — attached at entry when the intent carries a bracket, otherwise attached by the port's first amend, re-issued per bar and taking effect from the next bar's path, **except** that for `orders_on_close` legs a newly issued fixed stop/limit already crossed by the generating close fills at that close (`_evaluate_at_close`), which the rail realizes as a market `exit` with the triggering protection identity and FIFO lifecycle of S3(d) at the signal — the emulator's own rule evaluated on the bar the daemon already holds, not a new exit rule; (iii) trailing exactly as the port's `Bracket` parameters define it, realized by a native trailing stop (activation at fill ± activation ticks, then the traded extreme ∓ offset, effective stop the tighter of fixed and trailing); (iv) market orders at the signal. Two deviation classes are accepted, both measured in Phase 8 execution parity against frozen tolerances (`OWED-BY: TB-F1`): **latency** (an order reaches the broker seconds after the bar close instead of at `THIS_CLOSE`/`NEXT_OPEN`) and the **fill-path** class (the emulator's intrabar path assumption versus actual ticks for stops and trails — the TV-versus-live gap already inherent to the parity evidence). A semantic substitute — a daemon-held stop, a daemon-evaluated intrabar stop, a synthesized trail, protection on a lot the port left bare — is a different strategy and is never built. The separately ratified S6 emergency flats are operational risk-reduction exceptions, not claims of replay parity; all incident recovery is listener-owned under rev8; §5 authorizes the control/report amendment.

## §2 — Event sequences (component · state read/written · fail-closed outcome; TB-I3 drives each sequence end to end through the simulated broker and asserts every transition — test `test_seq_s<n>_*`; the concrete cases are §2d)

**S1 — market entry / add** (Aegis, Striker, Vanguard entry and add, ORB add). (1) adapter `on_bar` → `OrderIntent(kind, side, qty=<adapter-normal quantity>, bracket=<the port's bracket or None>)` (positive int; adapter units — R-P) [daemon; adapter state only]. (2) barrier collects the bar's completion events and releases in the RC-5 order [`barrier.py`]. (3) `emit_risk_add` [daemon; control read older than one bar or unreadable → no risk-add emission, `control_read_failed`; risk-reducing emissions are gated only by `emit_risk_reducing`]. (4) B1 payload `{leg_id, signal_type, bar_time, close, stop_dist_pts, side, qty_normal, stop_loss?, take_profit?, trail_activation_ticks?, trail_offset_ticks?}` — bracket fields **only when the intent carries a bracket**; **no `qty`** [`b1_payload.py` maps `OrderIntent.qty` → `qty_normal`; a `sell` to open for an Equity-Index leg is refused at build]. (5) listener admission in this order: test identity → `blocks` (any member → `policy_block`) → arming expiry → side vs `allowed_entry_side` and the hedging bar → dedupe (TB-I4) → lifecycle (`watch_tiers_zero`) → `mode` (`settled_date` ≠ prior venue session → halt) → quantity law (R-Q, exact rational, from `qty_normal`) → capacity (refuse-never-clip; Aegis may enter S10) → `L2` per the leg's declared protection cases (`LEG_MAP.protection_cases`, mirrored from the port's declaration): `bracket_at_entry` → (b), `bare_entry` → (f), `trailing` → (g), and (c), (d), (e) for every leg — any item not `supported` refuses the entry (`l2_refused`), independently of the daemon's boot-time check [listener; writes `pending=sent` + reservation **before** send]. (6) send with the attached bracket fields when present (`dry_run` → never) → `SendResult` [`pending=accepted`]. (7) broker evidence: fill / partial / reject; the intended bracket recorded at admission is checked against postdating `W`: components working (`attached_to` = the entry order) are marked so; an intended component absent from `W` within one bar of the fill is a `protection_gap` → recovery `CLOSE(fill)`; a bare fill has an empty intended set and is not a gap [`pending`, `P`, `W`, `ledger.confirmed`; the reservation is released on reject or cancel acknowledgement, converted on fill, and **held** while the outcome is unknown]. (8) `ExecutionEvent` served at `/executions?since=<cursor>` → adapter `on_execution` (the adapter learns the **executed** quantity here and sizes its exits from it) → checkpoint + cursor [daemon, atomic]. **Cuts:** no evidence within one bar → `pending=UNKNOWN`, reservation held, `blocks += unknown_order` — account-wide, because every admission reads the ledger that holds the unresolved reservation (I1) — until order-level postdating evidence (the terminal event, or `W` absence plus consistent `P`) resolves it.

**S2 — ORB stop entry (one branch).** Step 4 carries `stop_price` and the port's bracket (ORB's carries trailing parameters); step 5 admits the risk-add at the stop's quantity and holds the reservation while it rests; the listener sends `order_type=stop` with the bracket fields (`L2(a)`, `L2(b)`, `L2(g)` must be `supported`); `W[MNQ]` gains the order on evidence; it fills intrabar when crossed, exactly as the emulator fills it; a resting entry older than one bar is cancelled (RC-9, cancel acknowledgement releases the reservation) unless re-issued; a fill continues at S1 (7). The buy-only ORB port (D-B7) emits one resting entry per day, so two OCA siblings are never live; daemon-side OCA is not built (test: never two resting entries for one leg). **No fallback:** if `L2(a)` is not `supported`, the registry refuses to run the ORB leg and the live packet is `BLOCKED — capability-problem` (a market entry after the crossing bar closes is a different strategy from the qualified one — I8; D-B4 allows no substitute book).

**S3 — protection exactly as the port defines it (one branch per case).** (a) *Bracket at entry:* an intent with `bracket` set → the bracket rides the entry send (`L2(b)`) and `W` gains the protective orders **with the fill**. (b) *Bare entry, first protection later (Striker):* an intent with `bracket=None` fills bare — qualified behaviour (I8) — and the port's first `BracketAmend` for that lot becomes **`ATTACH`** (`L2(f)`): protection linked to the position from that moment; until then the lot is protected only by the EOD, kill, feed-loss and daemon-loss closes, exactly as in replay it is protected by nothing. (c) *Per-bar re-issue:* the ports re-issue their exit levels every bar as Pine re-calls `strategy.exit`; live that is **`AMEND`** per closed bar (`L2(c)`), applied at the broker seconds after the close, which the emulator models as the new level taking effect from the next bar's path (latency class). (d) *Close-time crossed level (`orders_on_close` legs, e.g. Striker):* when the port's newly issued **fixed** stop or limit is already crossed by the generating bar's close, the emulator fills the exit at that close; the **daemon** applies the same rule on the bar it already holds and emits a market `exit` for that scope (reason `stop_close` / `limit_close`) instead of the amend; the listener runs a durable `CLOSE` with explicit `triggered_protection` semantics and the triggering protection-owner identity: FIFO allocation, trigger consumption and sibling/anchor retention must match `_evaluate_at_close` → `_fill_exit`. It must not substitute an explicit lot-scoped close against the owner's originating fill. L2(d)/(e)/(g), as applicable, must qualify this realization; otherwise live qualification is blocked (latency class only after semantics match). An active native trail already through the close has executed at the broker intrabar, so no close-time trailing evaluation exists or is needed. (e) *Trailing:* the port's `trail_activation_ticks` / `trail_offset_ticks` travel in the bracket fields and are realized natively (`L2(g)`), OCO-linked with the fixed components; per-bar re-issue modifies only changed components, so an active trail's anchor is preserved exactly as the emulator preserves `trail_active` / `trail_extreme` across a bracket replacement; the daemon never tracks extremes. A tightening `AMEND` is risk-reducing (admitted under blocks, timeouts, late arrival); a loosening one is risk-adding (refused under any block). Rejection, unknown outcome and recovery follow the primitives (§1); sequential cancel/replace is never used. **No fallback:** a leg whose port uses a case whose `L2` item is not `supported` is refused at the registry and the live packet is `BLOCKED — capability-problem`; a daemon-evaluated intrabar stop, a synthesized trail or a bracket the port did not define is not the qualified strategy.

**S4 — cancel.** `cancel(order_ref)` from the adapter (ORB adds at a mode change through `set_mode`; stale resting entries) → the listener reads `pending[order_ref].kind` and fresh `W`: resting entry/add → risk-reducing → send, require the broker cancel acknowledgement, release the reservation; a resting entry/add with `pending = UNKNOWN` → the cancel is still sent (risk-reducing) and the acknowledgement or a "not working" reply is reconciled with postdating evidence before the reservation is released; protective → refused outside `CLOSE`/`AMEND` unless its position is `CONFIRMED(0)` on postdating evidence (orphan removal, risk-reducing); unknown ref → refused + alert.

**S5 — scoped exit / flat.** `exit` carries `qty` = the adapter's confirmed open quantity in scope (learned from fills, never from intent); the listener clamps `qty` **down** to the leg's confirmed allocation (never up; `exit_qty_clamped`) and runs **`CLOSE(fill scope)`**, which uses the verified `L2(d)`/`L2(e)` atomic reduction: protection remains effective for any unclosed remainder after each partial fill and is removed only when that scope is flat. Merely requesting a whole-fill close does not prove these properties; they require route evidence and partial-fill tests. A quantity-bounded close completes when the requested quantity is confirmed closed and the residual's protection is working at the intended parameters; a protection operation completes as consumed only when its protection owner is confirmed consumed under the K2 identity rule; FIFO exhaustion of the originating lot alone is insufficient. `flat` = `CLOSE(leg)`. Both are risk-reducing (admitted under every block, timeout and late arrival). Fill → `P`, `W`, `expected_protection`, `ledger`, `ExecutionEvent`.

**S6 — critical source/control/bar failure.** Apply rev8 §2 thresholds. Atomically enter HALTED and recover the whole book through §3. Source reports are health evidence, not B1 flat signals; the listener owns recovery. Late risk-reducing actions require valid scope and serialization with existing recovery. Source recovery, healthy bars and new sessions never grant permission. R-1 is the durable permission rule; R-2 is the common recovery owner. No per-source trading continuation mode.

**S7 — scheduled halt and flat.** Use rev8 §5: cutoff blocks risk-adds and cancels remainders; flatten start publishes every owned-symbol CLOSE scope before dispatch; preserve protective orders under the qualified primitives. Complete only on fresh E1–E3/K1 evidence and acknowledged disarm. At D, incomplete recovery is a deadline breach. Settle mode/reset accounting only from the required complete evidence; resetting accounting never clears HALTED. Missing calendar coverage halts, never invents a deadline.

**S8 — operator stop/resume.** Stop immediately enters HALTED and invokes rev8 §3 whole-book recovery. Status is read-only. Daemon acknowledgment is diagnostic. Recovery completes only with fresh account quiescence and acknowledged disarm. Resume is the distinct one-use, generation/boot/evidence-bound action in rev8 §4; it cannot clear unresolved work or override the schedule. No automatic resume.

**S9 — crash and restart (cut at every S1 step and every protection-operation send/outcome boundary).** *Listener:* restore `operations`, `expected_protection` and `last_control_read` first; resume/reconcile each unresolved `CLOSE`/`AMEND`/`ATTACH` using its original identity before any overlapping dispatch; never infer that a missing acknowledgement means no execution. Then rebuild `ledger` from `ExecutionStateStore` + `pending`; `blocks += restart_unreconciled`; require `P` **and** `W` `CONFIRMED` for every owned symbol through R-M (L-1) on evidence that postdates the restart; reconcile `pending` against `W`: an order in `W` absent from `pending` (orphan), or in `pending` as `sent`/`accepted` but absent from `W`, halts that leg with `restart_orphan_order` and an alert; **an orphan of unknown kind is never auto-cancelled** (it may be protection); a position whose `expected_protection` names an order absent from `W` is a `protection_gap` → recovery `CLOSE(symbol)`; a bare lot with an empty expected set is not a gap; the block clears only on a PASS reconciliation. *Daemon:* restore each adapter from its checkpoint, resume the cursor, consume outstanding events, then compare the adapter's open fills, its per-fill defined brackets and resting intents with `P` and `W` for its symbol; mismatch → that leg halts; no checkpoint → cold start holds emission for TB-W1's counts. A crash between S1 (6) and (7) leaves `pending=UNKNOWN` with its reservation held until evidence; between (7) and (8) the event is re-served from the cursor and applied once (I5). Permission starts HALTED regardless of restored config; reconciliation completion alone never resumes. Recover to flat and require rev8 §4 fresh operator approval. Retain all operation identities and evidence requirements above.

**S10 — capacity takeover (Aegis, D-B8).** Under RUNNING with valid authorization and `blocks = ∅`, atomically publish the takeover and every displaced scope before any dispatch (A1/K3): cancel every displaced resting **or partially filled** entry/add, counting a cancel only when evidence shows the order terminal (a route's accept of the request is not confirmation) → **`CLOSE(symbol)`** per displaced symbol → `P = CONFIRMED(0)` and the symbol's `W` empty on each, on postdating evidence → reconcile → re-run S1 admission against current blocks, policy, lifecycle, source/bar validity, capacity and fresh displaced-scope quiescence → admit Aegis only if all checks still pass; any unacknowledged cancel or partial/rejected/unknown/timed-out close refuses Aegis and preserves all confirmed partial broker effects, reservations and outstanding recovery owners; it never rolls back or assumes unchanged displaced positions: an unresolved close is `unknown_order`, an expected protective order missing after reconciliation is `protection_gap`, each cleared by its owner; no separate capacity block exists. Under O-5/O-6 the protected Striker ceiling is 77 micro-equivalents, so the protected path is reachable and tested.
