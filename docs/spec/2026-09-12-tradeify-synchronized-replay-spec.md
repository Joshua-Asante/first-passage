**RC-6 — marked equity (rev 3).** Per bar, account equity = cash + Σ legs open P&L at the bar close (EOD ratchet uses the last bar of the session); the floor test uses the **side-aware adverse mark**: a long leg at its adverse extreme, the short leg (Aegis) at its adverse extreme, summed across legs on the same bar (adverse extremes assumed coincident across legs; no favourable netting). Marking is **lifetime-scoped on the fill path**: each position contributes adverse P&L only over the portion of the bar during which it was actually open. Concretely, on an OHLC bar without tick data: (a) a position already open at the bar open (or opened at the open) is exposed to the full-bar adverse extreme (long → bar low, short → bar high), and if it also exits on that bar the conservative rule assumes the adverse extreme is reached before the exit fill — so a −10 excursion followed by a +20 take-profit still contributes −10; (b) a position opened mid-bar is marked only against the post-entry remainder of that bar's path (entry fill is the baseline; adverse excursion is the side-aware distance from entry to the adverse extreme of the remaining OHLC path after the fill); (c) a **close-only entry** (opened at the bar close) has empty pre-close lifetime on that bar, so its adverse contribution on the entry bar is **0** — it must not inherit the bar's earlier low/high as a pre-entry loss; its first markable adverse bar is the next one. The session's `intraday_low` is the minimum over its bars of (lifetime-scoped adverse mark − session opening equity), clipped at 0.

**RC-7 — coverage rule.** For each session and each leg, the leg's expected timestamps are the union grid of the four panels restricted to that leg's own session window (its ported Pine filter); a missing timestamp on **any** leg, in position or flat, excludes the session from the decision-bearing pool and is counted; last-close imputation is permitted only in diagnostic runs, labelled in every output.

**RC-8 — flatten legality (rev 3).** Each leg's own Pine EOD flat is replayed as ported (Aegis 16:00 ET signal → next-open fill; Vanguard 16:00 ET → 16:15; Striker and ORB at the close of the 15:45 / 16:15 bar), **and the replay models the rail's EOD scheduler exactly as TB-S3 R-G**: at the frozen buffer (16:30 ET regular, 12:44 ET early-close) it blocks new entries, cancels every pending order across all legs and submits a market `closeposition` flatten for any position still open. **Scheduler fill timing (load-bearing, identical to the rail):** the scheduler flatten is a market close at the **buffer instant**, stamped `fill_time = buffer` (16:30 ET regular / 12:44 ET early-close) with venue commission and slippage charged — it is **not** a next-bar fill. Pine strategy EOD flats keep their ported next-bar (or bar-close) timing; only the rail backstop uses buffer-instant market fills. That keeps `fill < 16:45 ET` / `< 12:59 ET` on the 15-minute grid without moving the frozen buffer earlier. The `AccountClock` then verifies every position is flat and no order is working strictly before the deadline (`fill < 16:45 ET`, `< 12:59 ET`). A remaining violation is a **qualification failure of that path** (counted as a failure outcome, T = ∞), never an exclusion; the 17:00–18:00 ET halt makes a next-open fill after 16:45 land at 18:00, which the check catches.

**RC-9 — duplicates, stale orders, partial fills.** An action whose `(leg, kind, bar_time)` already produced an accepted order is dropped; a resting entry/add older than one bar is cancelled and its reservation released regardless of position; the leg state machine is the adapters' `on_execution` path (confirmed fills only); the decision-bearing fill model is **full fill** (frozen field), with partial fraction as a stress cell; a partial exit leaves a residual that keeps its bracket and is flattened by the Pine's EOD path; a partial fill can never create a short.

## What the replay certifies and what it does not

Closes, item by item, the acceptance record's limits: (1) ORB adds-off exits are **native** (stall exit off, average-price exits recomputed); (2) sizing is **integer** under the ruled per-leg laws, not a continuous 40 %; (3) capacity, takeover and freed room are **recalculated natively**, not carried from the study's schedule; (4) equity is **marked per bar** with adverse excursion scoped to each position's lifetime inside the fill path (close-only entries contribute 0 on the entry bar), and the floor is tested on the side-aware adverse mark, not on daily cashflows; (5) the mode is chosen from the prior settled close on the **path**, with leg and account state carried continuously through the path's actual mode sequence, on a resampled clock that separates unique `path_time` from source-session calendars; (6) the rail's EOD scheduler is replayed with buffer-instant market flattens (not next-bar), so replay semantics equal rail semantics at the deadline. It does **not** certify: the TradingView margin branch (absent by design, O-7); contract-month seams (`ACCEPTED_UNMODELED`, obligations carried into TB-P1); real fills (Phase 8 and B7 own execution); and it is only as good as the frozen compute budget allows — a budget breach is `NEEDS_CONTEXT`, not an approximation.
