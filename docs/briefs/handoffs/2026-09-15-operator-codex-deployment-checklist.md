# Deployment checklist — operator and Codex responsibilities

Status: working checklist, 2026-09-15. Separates ownership; grants no new qualification, funding, merge, deployment or live authority. Current engineering checkpoints are still under combined review. Read the exact accepted contracts at execution; do not treat this summary as a replacement.

## How to use

- **Operator:** supplies facts accessible only through their account, controls private signing credentials, makes the named substantive decisions and performs attended trading-platform actions.
- **Codex:** researches, specifies, implements, collects authorized read-only evidence, verifies, tests, reviews and prepares concrete decision packets; runs gated tools once their prerequisites and authorization are established.
- **Joint:** Codex prepares the exact procedure and evidence packet; operator supplies attendance/credentials/signature/decision/action; Codex verifies the observed result.
- Missing external source facts are **evidence blockers**, not automatically operator mistakes or approvals waiting to be clicked. A signature cannot create a fact.
- Preserve existing approvals. No repeated E/P/U, attended-design or September-calendar ratification without a substantive change.
- Do not collect the fresh B7 seal until the release and final sequence are ready. Ordinary development does not consume its freshness window.
