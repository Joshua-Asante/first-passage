## 0.5. Frozen design decisions (operator-ruled 2026-09-21; constraints, not options)

**D1 — Custody.** FULL_E1 N1 capture, attestation and assessment live in **new versioned campaign tables**: DB `user_version` 8 with exact layout validation, snapshot `/v6` (a strict superset of `/v5`; budget-profile v3 ↔ snapshot coupling extended, not broken), and **one closed FULL_E1 checkpoint evidence family** — `qualification_campaign_checkpoint_result/v1`, `…_attestation/v1`, `…_assessment/v1` — produced and consumed together. `ExecutionStore.executions` stays N1_ONLY (its CHECK and the cross-capability collision rule are accepted invariants). `qualification/evidence.py` and `journal_snapshot.py` are versioned in the same change (a FULL_E1 assessment builder beside `build_n1_evidence`, a FULL_E1 assessment snapshot beside `parse_assessment_snapshot`); pinned-vector tests extended, never replaced.

**D2 — G5 launch.** G5 runs as a **host transient unit** (`User=g5_uid`) under the campaign's work slice, started by the guardian through the existing `campaign_control` prefix (`CAMPAIGN_BUS_START`), charged via its own cgroup against the `N1_G5` reservation, with `RuntimeMaxUSec`/CPU bounds derived from that phase exactly as `guardian_unit_spec` does for the guardian. No container. `campaign_host.cleanup`, `container_ownership.campaign_scopes` and the ownership/cleanup tests are taught the new unit kind (`…-g5.service` under the work slice). G5 talks to the service over the socket as the `g5` role with the new operations (§1).

**D3 — Worker output and handshake.** The real `worker` entrypoint uses the probe's handshake: `bootstrap.py` already installs and blocks SIGUSR1 for the `worker` role before any import; `worker.main` calls `block_resume_signal()` (token) and `await_resume()` before any work. Worker output leaves the container through a **bounded output mount owned by the work**: a tmpfs bind under the payload slice, size-limited to the profile's `output_byte_limit`, mounted read-write into the container at a fixed path, read by the guardian after `PAYLOAD_EXIT` and archived byte-for-byte (the N1_ONLY `docker start --attach` capture is not used). `BudgetGuard.from_contract` is replaced for FULL_E1 by a phase-limited guard supplied with the remaining N1 limits (`limits.cpu_ns − orchestration`, remaining wall, memory), and `parse_worker_result`/`verify_execution` compare observations against those same limits for FULL_E1 (N1_ONLY keeps the contract maxima). Changing `worker.py` changes the signed runtime closure and the worker image: record the new identities in §7.

**D4 — Release/profile.** One new literal `qualification_execution_release/v5` with profile `/v5`: `supported_checkpoints=['N1']`, `dispatch_enabled=True` **for N1 only** (an explicit `dispatch_checkpoints` field, closed set), `production_execution=False`, `authority_class=TEST_ONLY`; v4 and older keep refusing dispatch (`release_schema.py` :42 semantics preserved for them); fresh attempts only — no v3/v4 attempt is upgraded. The budget-profile v3 / snapshot v5 pair is untouched except by D1's `/v6` superset.
