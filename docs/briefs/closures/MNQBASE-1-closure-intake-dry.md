## §4 — The one external number worth carrying, and its defect

The falsification paper's headline is a **gross edge ceiling**: *"Across all fourteen signal families,
the maximum achievable gross return before friction is roughly 0.07 to 1.50 points per trade"* (§6.1
restates it as 1.05–1.50), against a 2.0-pt friction cost — with a structural reading in §6.2 that
liquid-contract OHLCV edge competes down to the marginal participant's friction.

**Do not cite that ceiling as a bound.** It is **internally inconsistent with the paper's own Table 11**,
which tabulates best-gross figures of **+4.82 pt** (ORB long, bar+15) and **+16.52 pt** (gap continuation
short) — both above the stated 1.50 ceiling. `lesson_borrowed_numbers_need_connecting_arithmetic`: the
error enters exactly at this transcription boundary, so the defensible external claim is the weaker,
non-numeric one:

> **0 of 14 standard OHLCV signal families cleared T≥2.0 + N≥30 + positive-net-after-friction +
> year-stability on MNQ.**

Applied at *our* basis (round trip **1.41 pt**, so T1 needs gross `G(s) = 0.40s + 1.41`), the paper's
**tabulated best** results still fall short across the Step-1 stop grid:

| Source result | gross | net @ our 1.41 pt | implied R @ s=20 | @ s=40 | T1 (0.40R)? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ORB long, bar+15 | +4.82 | +3.41 | 0.17R | 0.085R | ✗ |
