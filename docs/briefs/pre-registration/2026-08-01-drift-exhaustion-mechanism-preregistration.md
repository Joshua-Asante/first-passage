## §1 — The problem this design must solve, stated before anything else

**The hypothesis was generated by looking at the data.** Q-EODADV-1 pre-registered two mechanisms,
falsified both, and the *explanation* that fits — drift exhaustion — emerged from the residue. A
"pre-registration" that then confirms it on the same panel would be circular and worthless.

**Therefore the following are BURNED and may not serve as confirmation:**

- the MNQ exit-time ladder (Q-SESSCONF-1) — it is the symptom;
- the MNQ channel decomposition and hazard arithmetic (Q-EODADV-1) — they generated the hypothesis;
- `corr(r_rod, r_last)` on MNQ — same;
- **any MNQ statistic whatsoever**, because the hypothesis was selected for fitting MNQ.

**Confirmation must come from an instrument the hypothesis was not fitted to.** The primary test is
**MYM**, with the prediction *derived and frozen before* MYM's P&L is looked at. MNQ appears in this
brief only as a **consistency illustration**, never as evidence.

**Honest limitation, disclosed pre-result:** MYM and MNQ are correlated index futures
(`corr(RV) = 0.717`, cross-index probe 2026-07-21), so this is replication on a *related* instrument,
not an independent draw. It is the strongest test available from panels on hand. Two cross-asset
probes (XAUUSD, 6J) are **reported and non-gating** — a mechanism that is equity-index-specific is a
scope limitation, not a falsification.

## §2 — The mechanism, stated precisely enough to be wrong

For an intraday, trend-aligned position carrying a **fixed** stop and **no profit target**:

- **L1 — drift decay.** Conditional expected return per unit time, given the position is open and
  trend-aligned, **declines monotonically** from entry toward ≈ 0 across the session.
- **L2 — flat hazard.** Per-unit-time probability of touching the fixed stop is **approximately
  constant** — it does not spike late.
- **Joint.** Net per-minute contribution = `drift(t) − hazard × |damage|`. Since damage is fixed at
  ≈ −1R and hazard is flat, the net contribution **crosses zero at an interior time t\***, before the
  session close. Holding past `t*` is uncompensated risk.

This is deliberately *not* an end-of-day market story. It predicts the adversity is a property of
**position geometry against decaying edge**, not of the clock — which is why it must be testable on
an instrument whose session ends at the same time but whose edge profile differs.

## §3 — Question

**Q-DRIFTEX-1:** Is the late-session adversity of a trend-aligned, fixed-stop intraday position
produced by **drift decaying to zero against a roughly constant stop hazard** — and does that
structure reproduce on an instrument the hypothesis was not fitted to?

## §4 — Falsifiable hypothesis and frozen thresholds

**H-DRIFTEX-1 — If**, on **MYM**, (L1) conditional drift declines significantly across the session
**and** (L2) the late-session stop hazard is not elevated **and** (P3) the `t*` derived from L1+L2
**alone** locates MYM's own exit-time ladder maximum, **then** drift exhaustion is established as a
structural property and a *mechanism-derived* exit rule becomes licensable; **otherwise** it is not
established, and the 15:30 exit and every other early exit stay barred.

| Limb | Frozen threshold |
|---|---|
| **L1 — drift decay** | Regress per-30-min conditional drift (trend-aligned, position-open) on time-of-day. **Newey-West(5) slope t < −1.96.** |
| **L2 — flat hazard** | Observed final-block stop rate within **±25%** of a constant-hazard extrapolation fitted on 10:00→15:30. (MNQ illustration: −10%, comfortably inside — *illustration, not evidence*.) |
| **P3 — prediction locates the turnover** | `t*` computed from L1+L2 **before** the ladder is examined; **\|t\* − argmax(MYM ladder)\| ≤ 45 min**. |
| **Signal sufficiency (AMBIGUOUS guard)** | MYM ladder max must exceed its full-session cell by **≥ 0.10 annSR**; otherwise the ladder cannot test P3 and P3 is undecidable. |

**Accept H-DRIFTEX-1 if:** on MYM, NW(5) drift-vs-time slope **t < −1.96** AND final-block stop rate
within **±25%** of the constant-hazard extrapolation AND **|t\* − argmax(MYM ladder)| ≤ 45 min**, with
the ladder max exceeding its full-session cell by **≥ 0.10 annSR**.

**Reject H-DRIFTEX-1 if:** the drift slope is not significantly negative (**t ≥ −1.96**), **or** the
final-block hazard is elevated beyond **+25%**, **or** `t*` misses the ladder argmax by **> 45 min**
while the signal-sufficiency guard is satisfied.

**Ambiguous-hold if:** L1 and L2 pass but the MYM ladder max exceeds its full-session cell by
**< 0.10 annSR** — P3 is then undecidable on the available panel.

**Order of operations is part of the pre-registration.** L1 and L2 are measured, `t*` is computed and
**written into the RESULTS file**, and only then is the MYM ladder computed. A RESULTS artifact that
reports `t*` and the ladder in the same pass, without the frozen intermediate, is void.
