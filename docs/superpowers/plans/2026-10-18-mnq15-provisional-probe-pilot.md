## E1 — scheduled dividend-payment pressure, long MNQ

**Claim:** Larger previously announced ordinary cash dividends payable by Nasdaq-100 constituents predict greater positive intraday MNQ pressure through reinvestment. The constrained cash-share purchaser is plausible; participation, broker timing, offsetting flows and transmission to MNQ remain unverified. This is a predictive test of that hypothesis, not a measurement of actual DRIP orders.

**Point-in-time proxy:** At 09:15 ET on session t, compute `D_t = sum(w_i * cash_i,t / P_i)` across the Nasdaq-100 membership effective for t. Use the latest index weight and split-consistent reference close available by the prior regular-session close; ordinary USD cash dividends whose announced payable date maps to t; and only corporate-action notices published by 09:15 ET. Map a non-trading payable date to the next regular cash session as a declared timing assumption. Exclude specials; apply no later correction retroactively. Unknown membership, weight, price, dividend completeness or publication timestamp makes the day's proxy unavailable, never zero. Issuer notices/index records are proposed source classes, not already-bound datasets.

**Signal:** `D_t > 0` and strictly above the nearest-rank 75th percentile of the preceding 252 session values of D, each reconstructed from its own as-of information. With 252 values, use sorted observation 189. No current-day value enters its threshold. This is the only signal and threshold.

**Clock and order:** At the completed M15 boundary 09:15 ET, schedule one long entry for 09:30 ET if the signal is true and readiness passes. The execution model uses the 09:30 bar open with the frozen adverse cost convention. Missing that boundary cancels the entry; no catch-up entry, short, add or re-entry. Skip holidays and shortened cash sessions. Zero-trade days are normal.

**Protection and exit:** Compute simple-mean ATR from the last 20 completed expected M15 true ranges ending at 09:15, using the immediately preceding close for each true range. At entry set the fixed stop two ATR below the entry-bar reference open, rounded down to the MNQ tick. Stop level never moves; no target or trailing exit. A gap below the stop uses the worse opening price; otherwise a bar low touching the stop exits at the stop before costs. Flatten any survivor at the 15:45 bar open, before inspecting that bar's later range. Entry and exit costs are charged separately. Missing required bars or unusable stop geometry blocks the observation. M15 modelling supplies conservative research fills, not demonstrated native execution fidelity.

**Session state:** America/New_York trading-date key; archived CME and US cash-session calendars handle DST and holidays. Reset pending state each session; verify flat before a new entry; expire an unfilled entry at its scheduled boundary. Later execution must protect between bar closes. A firm whose permitted session cannot contain this template is placement-blocked; do not silently shorten the trade and call it E1.

**Risk unit:** One MNQ contract for measurement. Stop-risk, gap loss and fees are reported; no account balance or risk allocation is inferred. Future quantity admission uses the account owner's actual rules and may refuse the trade.
