## 4. Review and reporting contract

Every packet PR must answer five questions in its body:

1. Which authorization and K/dollar envelope permitted this packet?
2. Which outcome-bearing bytes were readable before the freeze?
3. What exact hashes and counts define the input, code, config, and reserve?
4. Which terminal rule fired mechanically?
5. What was deliberately **not** changed or attempted?

Do not update `STATE.md`, session logs, indexes, ledgers, and result narratives in separate waves
when one atomic packet can update the actual owners together. The objective is fewer reviewable
decision packets, not more repository transactions.

## 5. Definition of campaign completion

The campaign is complete when one row in §3 is reached and all owning artifacts agree. Success is
not “the pipeline ran.” Research success is one confirmed candidate. Tradeify success is that
candidate independently clearing the elected Tradeify edition. Programme success is a third,
separate axis. A clean, precommitted stop at Pilot, Attribution, Translation, or Confirm is an
honest completion—not permission to begin another near-neighbor campaign.
