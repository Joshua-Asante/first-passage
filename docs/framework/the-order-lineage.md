**What's new.** Mostly this is a composite: Shape Up, Registered Reports and Stage-Gate, Duke and Elster, appraised in Lakatos's terms. The new parts are narrow:
- (a) A counterparty-ask gate and an oracle deadline applied to *agent-generated* work, with inference banned from the evidence cell.
- (b) The premise that only brakes agents can't produce count.
- (c) **The empty quarantine.** This deliberately departs from Shape Up, whose cool-down is used to choose the next bets.
