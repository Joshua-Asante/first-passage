## 1. Objective and measure

Answer one question by 2026-12-31: is there an edge at all in the landed data? Progress is
measured in decisive results: registered cards that reach a verdict and whose mapped action
happens. The round succeeds if it ends in one Codex-verified CONFIRMED card or in a clean stop.

## 2. Scarce input and base rate

The scarce input is genuine edge after costs. Agents cannot produce more of it per dollar, so
agent work is paced to four cards, not to agent capacity. Comparable cases, from this
programme's own record: commit subjects since 2026-08-14 show 15 FALSIFIED, 3 NULL, 2 abandoned
and 0 confirmed; the builder's 16 cards on MCL and MGC all failed the same gate; DYN-SURVEY-1
run 1 was a partial null. The expected result of this round is no confirmed card.

## 3. Budgets and expiry

- New spend: $0. No data, compute or subscription is bought.
- Looks: at most **4 cards in total**, across all instruments, covering every development card
  and every holdout attempt. K = 4 is banked whether or not a card runs to the end. No
  replacement batches, no added instruments.
- Eligible instruments: only those with landed, hash-registered data and remaining lifetime
  looks (the index micros, MGC, M6E, 6J).
- All four cards are named in one registration before any exploration, chosen by stated
  rationale from existing dossiers and parked cards, never by outcome.
- A card must pass a cost re-read at the builder's frozen slip before it spends a look. A card
  that fails the re-read still counts against the four.
- DYN-SURVEY-1 is folded in: its Addendum 1 rerun takes card slots as open decision 2 settles.
- Expiry: 2026-12-31.

## 5. Stop conditions

The round stops, with no extension, on the first of:

- the fourth card reaching a verdict, or the four being spent in any other way;
- 2026-12-31;
- any proposal, by anyone, to loosen a gate, add a card or instrument, or reread a verdict.

Each card's registration maps outcomes to actions before it runs. CONFIRMED (under the frozen
gates, verified by Codex by a separate route) permits only a separate deployment decision,
under its own contract, after quarantine, with a payout model built first. FALSIFIED or NULL
closes the claim; the slot is spent and nothing is repaired. Each registration states the
chance of a false CONFIRMED at zero edge, and Codex re-derives it before the run.

When the round stops, a 14-day quarantine starts that day. The closing record is written in it.
No second round is drafted.
