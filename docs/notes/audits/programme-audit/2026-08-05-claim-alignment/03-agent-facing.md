## Why this section exists

Skills, agent configs, hooks, cursor rules and deployment runbooks are not documents a reader *chooses*. They are **auto-loaded on trigger words, ahead of any doc a human would elect to open**, and they are consumed *mid-task* — at the moment an action is being decided rather than at the moment a topic is being researched. A stale claim in `CLAUDE.md` misleads a reader who can still check it. A stale instruction in `.claude/skills/c1-rail/SKILL.md` or `deploy/c1_rail/README.md` **causes an action**.

Round 1's completeness critic independently flagged `.claude/` as its single largest coverage hole — *"the highest-leverage surface: skills load automatically on trigger words, ahead of any doc a reader chooses"* — and named `deploy/`, `PIPELINES.md` and `scripts/m1_item5_capture.py` in the same breath, all untouched by its twelve domains. Round 2 swept exactly those surfaces. It confirms the hole: **71 of round 2's 110 confirmed findings (64.5%) are agent-facing**, concentrated in `.claude` (23), `docs` (19), `scripts` (16), `deploy` (8) and `.cursor` (4).

**The standard applied to every Action cell below is stricter than accuracy.** A proposed action must make the instruction **safe to follow literally**. An agent does not infer a caveat, does not weigh a hedge against an imperative, and does not scroll past the first executable-looking step to find a warning. So: bars are stated as bars, not as context; a dead recipe is deleted with a tombstone rather than annotated; and a banner is placed **above** the imperative it intercepts, never below it.

## The one gate that would have caught six of these rows

Six rows in this section — **A23 (SE-04)**, **A33(a) (SP-12)**, **A38 (SP-09)**, **A41(b) (SP-08)**, **A45 (RDB-01)** and **A47 (LAB-6, 101 occurrences / 86 targets / 53 files)** — are the *same* defect: a **path selector broken by a relocation**, in every case the 2026-08-03 theme nest or the `ops/c1_rail/` move at `2345095`. Three constraints on that proposal, all of them earned in the rows above and none of them optional:

1. **It is a gate-scope change and needs its own ADR** — not an audit edit (SP-12's own class note says so).
2. **It must resolve archived studies to their `lab/analysis/<slug>/CARD.md` stub**, never to a theme directory, and must not treat `lab/archive/` or `docs/ltm/` as scannable *sources* — only as valid targets.

Fixing the path is the repair; widening the selector is the regression.
