**The protocol obligation.** The meta layer's verdict is **`DEGENERATING`**, and the Programme Audit Protocol requires that a `Degenerating` verdict name at least one action with an owner and a date. Rows **FU-5**, **FU-6**, **FU-7**, **FU-8**, **FU-12** and **FU-13** are therefore **mandatory outputs** of that verdict's seven evidence limbs, not optional suggestions — each names the limb it discharges. **FU-17** and **FU-18** are round-2 additions on the same footing: they discharge limbs 1 and 2 on independent evidence the round-1 verdict did not have.

**Ordering is by urgency, and urgency is not consequence.** The two are separated on purpose, in §7.3 and again in §7.4, because conflating them is how the highest-consequence item ends up behind a queue of checkpoint-dated rows. Round 1 made that inversion explicit for **FU-2**; FU-2 is now fixed, and §7.4 records where the inversion moved.

**Evidential standing.** Rows carrying round-2 evidence are marked **`(R2)`**. **Status.** Every open row is a **RECOMMENDATION pending operator ruling**. The four closed rows are the exception and say so.
