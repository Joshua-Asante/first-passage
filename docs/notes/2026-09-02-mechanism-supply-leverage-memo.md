## Executive answer

First Passage does not primarily have a backtesting-capacity problem. It has a **conversion
problem**:

> scarce mechanism prior → complete trade expression → trustworthy measurement → positive net
> edge → survivable Tradeify path → executable edition

Every arrow is conjunctive. More supply at the left edge has little value when it arrives as an
unsigned predictor, an incomplete sketch, or another close relative of an exhausted OHLCV
family. Conversely, more validation machinery has zero value while no complete expression reaches
it.

The highest-leverage move is therefore to change the unit of sourcing from **interesting
mechanism** to **evidence-backed, complete trade object**, while using the cheapest available
candidate-specific falsifier before doing general mechanism science. The newly recorded
`ORB-MYM v0.4 + P50 opening-range volume gate` is the nearest such object: its fully viewed
TradingView panel reports better net P&L, profit factor, and drawdown than both the ungated control
and P80, but it lacks retained trades, configuration proof, day-level shape, and an untouched
sample. It is a **lead**, not a candidate.

The 2026-09-02 immediate sequence below is **historical advisory**. The live campaign instruction
is VOLREGIME translation at Packet T, not P50 reconstruction (banner above). Do not re-elect
step 2–4 as the next action.

The 2026-09-02 sequence was:

1. decide the commercial target (`Select`, any Tradeify tier, or portable edge);
2. capture the P50 expression and full List-of-Trades export, then compute its day-level payoff
   shape and Tradeify reachability without testing another threshold;
3. if and only if that cheap reconstruction is plausibly reachable, retain P50 as a development
   prior and seek the separate legacy-intake ruling required before exact P50 can enter any
   prospectively valid candidate path;
4. do **not** spend roughly 703 core-hours on general L5 attribution merely to earn permission to
   ask whether P50 pays—the direct expression is now the shorter decision path;
5. in parallel, replace broad mechanism ideation with a bounded acquisition brief for complete,
   externally evidenced expressions.

No current evidence licenses capital, lifecycle promotion, or a claim that P50 clears Tradeify.

This does not require an idea to arrive fully formed. A mechanism or partial expression may be
**cultivated** on development evidence: missing rules can be supplied, an existing strategy can
gain a conditioner, and its economics can be improved iteratively. The hard boundary is later: the
object must become complete and immutable before it claims candidate status or touches untouched
confirmation.

## 1. The problem in its most basic elements

### Element 1 — The objective is not singular

Four distinct outcomes have been compressed into “find a Tradeify strategy”:

1. **Edge:** a complete expression has positive net expectancy on untouched evidence.
2. **Edition fit:** an integer-sized implementation clears a particular Tradeify tier's drawdown,
   pass, consistency, activity, session, and contract rules.
3. **Account operation:** inactivity preservation, monitoring, rail, and attended execution are
   acceptable to the operator.
4. **Programme discharge:** the strategy also contributes to the separate multi-firm objective.

These are ordered but not equivalent. A confirmed edge can fail Select and remain commercially
valuable; a governance change can help programme discharge without creating edge. Until the
operator names the primary objective, research cannot optimize the right reachability boundary.

### Element 2 — Supply has been counted too far upstream

The repository has produced signals, conditioners, effect sketches, and mechanism hypotheses.
Those are **inputs**, not candidate supply. Candidate supply exists only when one immutable object
specifies at least:

- instrument and session;
- observable and causal observation time;
- side rule and entry order/fill semantics;
- hard stop, target or exit, and maximum holding period;
- no-trade and simultaneous-signal behavior;
- integer sizing policy;
- cost/slippage authority;
- expected cadence, win/loss shape, clustering, and intraday MAE; and
- development and untouched confirmation partitions; and
- an independent mechanism discriminator with a frozen statistic, null, expected direction,
  decision threshold, and coverage/power requirement.

B1 demonstrated the distinction: source liveness could not repair absent expectancy, stop, exit,
and sizing. Q-VOLREGIME L1–L4 demonstrates it again: next-bar **range** information has no native
side and therefore no automatic expectancy.

### Element 3 — Tradeify viability is first-passage geometry, not Sharpe alone

The strategy must reach a profit target before a path-dependent loss barrier while satisfying
soft consistency and activity conditions. Consequently, mean expectancy is necessary but not
sufficient. The load-bearing state variables are:

- net edge per trade after realistic crossing costs;
- loss size, loss clustering, and gap-through-stop behavior;
- intraday MAE, not close-to-close P&L;
- cadence and time-to-target;
- integer risk and contract caps; and
- the chosen tier's drawdown rope and rule edition.

This explains why attractive pooled effects and early combined-book headlines have repeatedly
failed later. The shape map says the practical Select sourcing target is often a comfortably high
win rate with bounded losses; it does **not** say that maximizing win rate alone works. A high-win,
rare-large-loss process is especially hostile to a trailing barrier.

### Element 4 — Measurement integrity precedes statistical sophistication

Recent reversals came from upstream session, clock, sizing, and intraday-path semantics. If a
primitive is wrong, multiplicity controls and Monte Carlo faithfully evaluate the wrong object.
The minimum trustworthy measurement chain is:

1. source identity and configuration hash;
2. executable session/clock fixtures;
3. event- or trade-level export;
4. deterministic aggregation to the candidate's risk unit;
5. costs, fills, and intraday excursion;
6. untouched outcome access; then
7. statistical and venue evaluation.

This is why screenshots can establish a prior but cannot establish a candidate verdict.

### Element 5 — The current bottleneck is translation, not evaluation capacity

The estate already has survivor scoring, Tradeify rules, deployment infrastructure, and extensive
governance. What it lacks is a steady arrival process for complete expressions that plausibly fit
the target before expensive work begins. The production function is therefore approximately:

`viable output = complete-expression arrivals × true-edge rate × measurement survival × venue-fit rate`

The present complete-expression arrival rate is near zero. Increasing downstream rigor cannot
raise that first factor. Broadening upstream idea count with proxy-only objects does not raise it
either.

## 3. Highest-leverage solutions, ranked

### 1. Make the commercial objective explicit before more research — resolved

**Leverage:** very high. **Cost:** one operator decision. **Why:** it changes every downstream
reachability judgment and prevents the research process from silently alternating among Select,
Growth, portable-edge, and programme-discharge objectives.

Record:

1. primary objective: `Tradeify Select` / `any Tradeify tier` / `portable confirmed edge`;
2. whether a manual weekly preservation trade is acceptable as a permanent account-level control;
3. maximum calendar time and compute/data spend for the next decision; and
4. whether failure at the chosen edition preserves the edge for another venue/tier.

**Operator election, 2026-09-02:** optimize for a portable confirmed edge, prefer Select, report
alternate Tradeify editions separately, and permit manual weekly preservation as an account-level
control. Preserve a confirmed edge after edition failure. The 2–3 day clock now binds the campaign.

### 2. Convert the P50 source read into a cheap, falsifiable candidate-shape packet

**Leverage:** highest empirical value per unit cost. **Cost:** operator export time plus local
analysis. **Why:** it asks the direct economic question on the nearest complete expression before
funding a general attribution programme.

Before another Strategy Tester comparison is opened, retain:

- Pine body and hash;
- symbol, timeframe, date window, and all Strategy Properties;
- weekday toggles, pyramiding, quantity, costs, and slippage;
- complete List-of-Trades export; and
- the `Off`, `P50`, and `P80` setting identities already read—without adding thresholds.

Then aggregate entries/adds/exits to the actual position/day risk unit and print:

- N, cadence, and idle-week distribution;
- net expectancy and uncertainty after repository-authoritative Tradeify costs;
- win rate, mean/quantile win and loss, consecutive-loss runs, and worst day;
- MAE if reconstructible; otherwise an explicit `UNSCREENABLE` limb rather than an EOD proxy;
- integer-size risk and contract-cap feasibility; and
- a coarse, explicitly exploratory Tradeify shape/MC pre-check.

**Hard stop:** if the day-level object cannot plausibly clear cost, bounded-loss, activity, or
integer-size geometry under generous assumptions, close it before a forward campaign. Do not
repair it with P55, weekday retuning, a different stop, or a new exit.

### 3. If reachable, freeze one P50 forward test; do not manufacture a retrospective holdout

**Leverage:** high, but calendar-bound. **Why:** every historical threshold cell is viewed. New
bars—not a re-sliced old panel—are the cleanest remaining evidence.

The exact P50 expression cannot receive a forward contract under the current campaign: its
Off/P50/P80 selection was already outcome-viewed before any contract/K freeze. Retain its source
configuration and development evidence, but require either (a) a separate operator-ratified
legacy/source-intake decision that explicitly reconciles that selection, or (b) a genuinely
distinct successor prospectively frozen before payoff access. Paper/shadow execution is not a
substitute for the missing prospective selection controls.

For any admissible successor, commit the Confirm boundaries prospectively but start the interval
strictly after the founding-freeze commit; bars that arrive between the source read and that commit
are retrospective. The contract must also freeze the independent mechanism discriminator, because
payoff confirmation alone cannot produce `CONFIRMED` under the terminal taxonomy.

The precommitted horizon should be set from power and expected cadence, not impatience. If the
required horizon is commercially too long, that is information about the candidate's value—not a
reason to lower the bar midstream.

### 4. Reprice Q-VOLREGIME L5 as research infrastructure, not the shortest strategy route

**Leverage:** potentially high scientifically, low for immediate Tradeify conversion.
**Why:** a successful L5 result certifies incremental range information; it still does not supply
direction, stop, exit, or positive net expectancy. Meanwhile a failed pilot can consume hundreds
of core-hours and end only with machinery parked.

Therefore:

- do not run L5 merely to “permit” evaluation of an already specified P50 expression;
- run it only if its reusable scientific value across future mechanisms justifies its full
  operator-attention and compute cost;
- if run, preserve its existing one-shot design and pilot gates—do not cheapen the science to
  make it fit an urgency narrative; and
- keep its result orthogonal to P50, because opening-range aggregate volume and M15 trigger-bar
  volume are not identical constructs.

This is a sequencing change, not a claim that attribution is unimportant.

### 5. Change the sourcing unit: acquire complete expressions, not more raw mechanisms

**Leverage:** high on the true bottleneck. **Cost:** bounded sourcing/operator outreach.

Issue a narrow intake brief or bounty whose deliverable is a reproducible candidate contract, not
an idea. Minimum admission package:

- economically motivated, exact rules with no discretionary blanks;
- independently evidenced positive **net** expectancy or a credible large effect prior;
- sufficient cadence and sample size;
- hard loss containment and evidence about adverse excursion/tails;
- executable on MNQ/MYM or another Tradeify-legal product at integer size;
- raw trades or code sufficient for independent reproduction; and
- an untouched interval that First Passage can reserve.

Rank channels by their probability of delivering that package: audited third-party system/code
licensing, researcher or trader partnerships with exportable ledgers, and replication packages
with executable rules should outrank another general literature sweep. Reject signal services,
screenshots without trades, parameter menus, and “promising indicators” at intake.

Cap the burst by **complete admissible packages reviewed**, not citations visited. If none arrive,
accept that the acquisition channel is dry rather than converting weak submissions into internal
research projects.

### 6. Collapse progress reporting to one decision packet per empirical object

**Leverage:** medium-to-high because operator attention is scarce. Preserve the controls, reduce
the surface:

1. one candidate contract;
2. one data/config acceptance record;
3. one executable results packet;
4. one adversarial pre-outcome review; and
5. one terminal disposition.

Mirrors and indexes should be mechanically derived where possible. Count progress in new complete
expressions reaching untouched confirmation, not PRs, documents, or upstream effects discovered.

## 5. Stop doing

- Do not treat source availability, predictor significance, or range forecasting as candidate
  supply.
- Do not add another ORB threshold, weekday combination, stop, target, or entry variant to the
  fully viewed MYM panel.
- Do not force each alpha leg to solve an account-level inactivity policy unless the operator has
  explicitly rejected manual/companion preservation.
- Do not let a Growth result answer a Select question, or a firm-count ruling answer an edge
  question.
- Do not spend large compute merely because it is the next box in an existing plan; price the
  decision the result can actually change.
- Do not reopen dead OHLCV families to keep the queue non-empty.
- Do not confuse an honest dry result with process failure. A dry acquisition burst plus a failed
  P50 forward test would be a commercially useful stop signal.

## Bottom line

The basic failure is not that First Passage cannot evaluate strategies. It is that the supply
system usually hands evaluation something other than a strategy. Tradeify then compounds the
problem by demanding a narrow path shape, especially on Select.

The most leveraged response is not another broad search and not another layer of gates. It is to
make the target edition explicit, reconstruct the one current expression-level lead at the proper
risk unit, give it one honest forward chance if it is shape-reachable, and source future work only
as complete evidence-backed trade objects. If those routes remain dry, change the venue/objective
or pause. That is a better outcome than manufacturing a candidate from an unsigned mechanism or a
fully viewed parameter surface.
