**Status:** research input to the 11-08 sitting, branches (a) and (b) of the [deployment-checklist addendum 2026-10-13](../superpowers/plans/2026-09-20-tradeify-deployment-checklist.md#addendum-2026-10-13--track-b-suspended-11-08-decision-rules-draft). It changes no status and grants nothing. Track B stays suspended.
**Question:** within Tradeify's written guidance, how could the accepted four-leg book (Aegis 6J, Striker MYM, Vanguard MGC, ORB MNQ) be automated or semi-automated on a Tradeify evaluation or sim-funded account, and what would each route cost and need?

Common to every route: §6.6 automated bots ([Funded Trader Agreement](https://tradeify.co/funded-trader-agreement) §6.6 and the [Guidelines](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10468318-guidelines-for-traders)): "sole owner of the bot or strategy"; "While the Trader may use the bot on the Trader's personal accounts, using it across multiple firms is against Tradeify's policy"; "a live video of you enabling the code on your own PC"; no HFT. The written terms therefore expressly allow use on personal accounts, subject to those conditions. Support's 10-08 reply ("used only within Tradeify", [10-10 replies](2026-10-10-tradeify-exclusive-use-replies.md#3-answers-r1-paraphrased-per-point)) and that note's labelled inference conflict with this. Which governs a personal account running the same book is an **unresolved interpretation**, not a categorical prohibition. Nothing here establishes permission for any deployment configuration. Separately, §6.7 and the Guidelines' hedging rule (Product Group table: [Rules: Hedging & Correlated Products](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10495868-rules-hedging-correlated-products), updated 2026-08-19) prohibit opposing positions "on the same instrument or on two products from the same Product Group", in one account or across accounts, and a violation affects "every account involved". MYM and MNQ are both in the Equity Index group, so different symbols do not rule out a prohibited hedge. Whether the book ever holds opposite MYM/MNQ positions at once is not assessed here; it needs evidence from the position histories.

## Route 1: native NinjaScript (fixed parameters)

**Fit.** [G] says "all strategy logic, calculations and signal generation must be native to a supported platform: NinjaTrader (typically NinjaScript)". A strategy that computes every bar, signal, size, DD-tier state and order inside NinjaTrader meets those words literally. Tradeify's [NT8 setup guide](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/12268716-ninjatrader-8-setup-guide) adds: "Users are only able to trade their Tradeify accounts under Tradeify's company license with NinjaTrader. Users are NOT able to use their personal license". Buying a NinjaTrader licence (Monthly $99, Lifetime $1,499, [NinjaTrader pricing](https://ninjatrader.com/futures/blogs/how-much-does-ninjatrader-cost)) therefore does not help on this account. Whether the company licence enables automated NinjaScript strategies is **UNKNOWN**. Third-party reports that free licences exclude live automation describe live brokerage keys, not a prop sim connection (lead only).

**Offline parameters (interpretation).** Parameters frozen from offline research are inputs, not live "calculations and signal generation". Read that way, a fixed, release-bound parameter file fits [G], and most native strategies ship with tuned inputs. Anything recomputed off-platform during operation would be external calculation and would breach [G]. That covers a Python-produced daily size vector or DD state. The DD tier ([`core/dd_protection.py`](../../core/dd_protection.py); the Track B policy is the fixed 1% trigger / 40% scale per [STATE](../../STATE.md)) and LegSize sizing must therefore run in C# from native account equity. Tradeify has not confirmed this reading; it is follow-up question 3.

**Expressibility and evidence.** NinjaScript can hold the four legs' state, resting stop entries, brackets, per-fill trails and an account-level owner (#807 §2.1–§2.5). Current qualification evidence (T00/FLOOR-HALF, E1) runs on the Python ports. None of it transfers until a frozen identical-input comparison shows decision and sizing parity, plus native feed and fill equivalence (#807 §2.6, §6). The C# source would be an executable port of locked logic, so it stays private ([AGENTS.md public-clone posture](../../AGENTS.md#public-clone-posture)).

**CME.** An automated strategy is non-display, Category A, use ([CME note §1](2026-10-10-cme-non-display-licensing-research.md#1-licence-route-and-category)). Tradeify includes "Level 1 CME data" ([supported platforms](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10468221-supported-platforms)), but that does not settle the licence. Schedule 5 §3.3's non-professional carve-out conflicts with Subscriber Addendum §4.2's "requires a direct license" ([operator read, 10-12](2026-10-10-cme-non-display-licensing-research.md#addendum-2026-10-12--operator-read-of-the-licence-clauses)). Information Policies §7.3 also bears on prop simulations. Cost range: $0 if the vendor carve-out applies, up to $1,371/mo (User Non-Display, three exchanges: CME, CBOT, COMEX). CME ticket #04738415 is open.

**Operator time and risk.** Time: attended start and HALTED boot per session, monitoring, and the §6.6 video on request (estimate: under 30 min per session once qualified, plus the build). **Main risk:** multi-week to multi-month engineering on an unconfirmed reading (company licence, offline parameters, CME), followed by a parity requalification that may change results.

## Route 2: semi-automation with native tools

**Fit.** The operator places each entry by hand on Tradovate or NinjaTrader. That is ordinary manual trading and needs no [G] permission. Signals would come from platform-native chart indicators, alerts or the operator's own reading. **Interpretation:** a Python screen or sizing sheet consulted live would be an "external program … generat[ing] signals" or "calculations". Sizing and the DD tier therefore have to be done by hand or natively.

**Expressibility.** Tradovate has no strategy engine, so entries are manual. Its custom indicators are JavaScript chart calculators ([guide](https://tradovate.github.io/custom-indicators/)); no order capability is documented. Tradovate ATM strategies place "Stop Loss and Profit Target orders once an Entry order is executed", with OCO and seven stop types, including "Auto Breakeven", "Auto Trail" and "Auto BreakEven + AutoTrail" ([Tradeify Tradovate order-entry guide](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/13196343-tradovate-order-entry-copy-trading)). ORB's resting stop entry with fixed brackets fits. Vanguard's and Striker's per-fill trails, breakeven and ratchets map only approximately to ATM profit-trigger trails, and the [09-25 note](2026-09-25-tradingview-signal-route-evaluation.md) table lists the bar-boundary semantics they would miss. Aegis's bar-boundary target re-pin is a manual amendment.

**Cost, CME, evidence.** No platform fee ("no surcharge for any platform"; Level 1 CME included). CME (**inference** from the operator's 10-12 read of FAQ 30): a non-professional's semi-automated use is licensed through the vendor, not directly. Human order entry from a display is display use. Evidence: manual execution is a changed expression, and qualification on Python ports does not cover human latency, misses or rounding.

**Operator time and risk.** Time: present for every leg's signal windows across the trading day (estimate: several hours a day). **Main risk:** operator attention and execution error make the book a different, unqualified strategy. Hours are the binding constraint in [STATE](../../STATE.md).

## Route 3: TradingView via the Tradovate integration

**Fit.** [G] names "TradingView via the Tradovate integration". TradingView's own support says: "Strategy trading is limited to the backtesting mode only. Automated strategy trading with a brokerage account is not available on TradingView yet" ([TradingView support](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000481026/)). Without a webhook bridge, which [G] excludes, a Pine strategy cannot place broker orders. The route therefore reduces to Pine alerts plus manual orders through the Trading Panel. Tradeify: the "integration is free for all Tradeify users"; the free TradingView plan updates "once every 5 seconds", and full live updates need "any package" ([setup guide](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10468208-how-do-i-trade-with-tradingview), dated 2026-04-07). Essential is $12.95/mo billed annually ([pricing](https://www.tradingview.com/pricing/)); the monthly-billed price was not captured.

**ToS.** TradingView Terms §3 license content "for exclusive display-only use" and prohibit "any form of automated trading" and "machine-driven processes that do not involve the direct, human-readable display" ([policies](https://www.tradingview.com/policies/)). This matches the repo's prior reading ([S2b option C](../adr/2026-08-08-s2b-signal-daemon-build.md#option-c--tradingview-alert-webhook-as-bar-transport), Q3 ruled applicable). A human reading an on-screen alert and clicking an order is display use (**interpretation**). An agent doing it is OPEN (route 4).

**Evidence and risk.** Pine is the locked origin of the accepted ports, so signal logic needs no translation. However, Pine's simulated position and fills diverge from the account after any refusal, partial fill, broker-first exit or manual close ([09-25 note §1–§2](2026-09-25-tradingview-signal-route-evaluation.md)). Pine also cannot see real-account equity for the DD tier. **Main risk:** silent Pine/account state divergence under manual execution, plus route 2's operator-hours load.

## Route 4: agent-placed orders on the operator's computer

AGENTS.md lets agents place, exit and cancel orders "when directed by the operator", including through computer use ([live-execution posture](../../AGENTS.md#live-execution-posture)). [G] bars external programs that "generate signals" and bars bridges and APIs. An agent that only executes native signals, through the platform UI, at the operator's direction, generates nothing and uses no API. Read literally, that may fall outside both bars (**interpretation**; Tradeify's view is **UNKNOWN**; it is the drafted follow-up question 4). §6.6's "enabling the code on your own PC" and its HFT ban suggest Tradeify judges by ownership and behaviour. A screen-reading agent on TradingView is a "machine-driven process" under ToS §3 (**OPEN**), and TradingView login automation stays prohibited ([S2](../adr/2026-08-07-loop-s2-signal-host-fork.md)). **Main risk:** Tradeify treats it as automation by an external program after the fact, putting the account in violation. Do not use it before a written answer.

## Route 5: other supported platforms

Tradeify's [supported-platforms page](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10468221-supported-platforms) also lists Rithmic-side Quantower, Sierra Chart ("requires a paid Sierra Chart subscription"), R|Trader and Tradesea, plus WealthCharts. Some of these have their own strategy APIs. [G] names only NinjaTrader, Tradovate and TradingView, and the page says "you cannot switch between brokers … without purchasing a new account". New evaluations are frozen under the suspension. Partner bridges that vendors market for Tradeify (TradersPost, PickMyTrade, CrossTrade) are third-party bridges and excluded by [G].
