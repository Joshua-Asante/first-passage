# Progress-producing research methods — portfolio synthesis

**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** `SYNTHESIS — operating recommendation; no strategy admission or gate change`  
**Question:** What is common to the research methods that produced real progress, how should the
programme lean into those advantages, and how should it separate decision-changing work from
wheel-spinning?

## 1. Executive answer

The common factor is **not a particular signal family, statistical technique, or data source**.
Progress occurred when an investigation was built as a short, auditable path from a specific
uncertainty to a specific decision:

1. name the decision and its consumer;
2. start with a credible economic prior and a complete tradable expression;
3. apply venue, cost, and payoff-shape constraints before expensive inference;
4. freeze a two-sided gate before reading the outcome;
5. run the cheapest test capable of changing the decision;
6. challenge survivors with independent realism checks; and
7. stop, route, or promote immediately when the gate fires.

This pattern produced useful positive results, such as ORB-MNQ-1's native-futures transfer and
cost-law clearance, and useful negative results, such as eliminating an entire five-lead harvest
without paid extension. Both are real progress because each changed the feasible action set. By
contrast, activity spun its wheels when it established an interesting fact with no downstream
consumer, substituted data availability for expectancy, tuned after seeing outcomes, or created
another artifact without changing a decision.

The programme's comparative advantage is therefore best described as **high-integrity,
constraint-aware elimination and validation**, not unconstrained idea generation. Lean into that
advantage—but feed it better raw material. The current bottleneck is candidate quality: the next
source must arrive with a confidently high positive-net-expectancy prior and a sufficiently
complete expression to be falsified without first inventing the strategy.

**2026-09-02 recent-PR check:** PRs #258 and #259 sharpen, rather than overturn, this answer. PR
#258 shows that independent review is itself a progress-producing research method when it tests
the decision semantics and reproducer, not just prose. PR #259 shows that a small, already-built
source-stage comparison can make legitimate incremental progress before full candidate admission,
provided its license is narrow: select one setting for a genuinely new forward test, stop tuning
the viewed panel, and do not call the result confirmation. Sections 2.7 and 2.8 incorporate these
two cases.

## 2. What the progress-producing methods have in common

### 2.1 They begin with a decision, not a topic

The strongest studies specify what a pass and fail will do. The six-lead pursuit declared its
cheap falsifiers asymmetric: failure killed a sleeve, while passage licensed only a priced
extension. Q-BOOKFIT-1 similarly used an optimistic upper-bound screen: failure would exclude a
leg, while passage meant only “not excluded,” not “confirmed.” This asymmetry prevents a weak
screen from silently becoming an admission test.

**Common feature:** the output has a named route—kill, hold, buy data, enter the next gate, or
change an operational implementation. “Learn more” is not itself a disposition.

### 2.2 They test economic reachability before statistical subtlety

Stage-2 cost law separated ORB-MNQ-1 from D5 and H-OD-1 before the full confirmation battery. The
shape-feasibility map likewise quantified the venue's binding trailing-drawdown geometry before
asking Phase B to source mechanisms. In the six-lead plan, deduplication, venue legality, data
availability, cadence, and cost arithmetic preceded paid pulls and Confirm work.

**Common feature:** first ask whether a perfect estimate could matter. If the effect cannot clear
cost, minimum contract size, stop geometry, session legality, power, or account survival, better
estimation cannot rescue it.

### 2.3 They freeze the test and preserve provenance

The productive analyses parse frozen thresholds, reuse the production simulation path, retain
seed/horizon definitions, disclose reduced-N deviations, label marginal results, and distinguish
screen evidence from admission evidence. This creates an audit trail that can survive later
correction rather than a persuasive one-off narrative.

**Common feature:** the result is reproducible against canonical inputs, and uncertainty near a
gate is carried as uncertainty—not rounded into a pass.

### 2.4 They use staged, asymmetric falsification

Cheap arithmetic, deduplication, proxy validity, source feasibility, cost dry-runs, and small-N
screens are most valuable as **kill tests**. Survivors alone earn richer data, larger simulations,
or operator attention. The stage order matters: it converts cheap agent capacity into saved
operator attention and avoided spend.

**Common feature:** each stage has permission to stop the branch, while permission to proceed is
narrow and explicit.

### 2.5 They make survivors endure orthogonal realism checks

ORB-MNQ-1 progressed because the mechanism transferred to the native instrument where a literal
locked-Pine transplant did not, then cleared temporal, placebo, cost, and firm-realism checks.
Conversely, the Aegis×ORB combined-book headline disappeared only after tail-risk-consistent
sizing, both-halves testing, and intraday-honest sequencing were compounded. A single favorable
aggregate was never treated as enough.

**Common feature:** confirmation attacks a different failure mode from exploration—cost, regime,
tail shape, timing, placebo, or realized correlation—not merely the same hypothesis with more
rows.

### 2.6 They treat correction and null results as outputs

The overnight-range look-ahead correction reversed a headline while leaving an honest audit trail.
Phase B's zero-candidate terminal state is also a designed result: the plan explicitly bars
reopening a dead lane merely to avoid an empty queue. These outcomes reduce false confidence and
future search space.

**Common feature:** the system rewards accurate belief revision and branch closure, not the number
of positive findings.

### 2.7 They use review to test the experiment's semantics, not merely its presentation

PR #258's review found that a proposed compute optimization changed the sensitivity of gating
studies, introduced an escalation that contradicted the frozen disposition, reported wall time as
core time, shipped a reproducer for a rejected design, asserted rather than measured memory, and
generalized a cache benchmark beyond the model actually measured. All seven findings were
accepted; two invalidated the central proposal, and the claimed saving retreated from 83% to 45%.

That is real progress: the review prevented an efficient-looking but decision-changing shortcut
from becoming the experiment. It also preserved the genuinely output-identical optimization and
the reduced precision on the one explicitly non-gating cell.

**Common feature:** productive review asks “could this alter the verdict, resource authorization,
or claimed reproducibility?” It accepts retreat when the answer is yes. Review that only polishes
language after those questions remain unanswered is not the same method.

### 2.8 They allow small exploratory decisions without laundering them into confirmation

PR #259 compared `Off`, `P50`, and `P80` on an existing private ORB-MYM expression. P50 improved
the displayed net P&L and profit factor while roughly halving displayed drawdown versus `Off`; P80
showed that the relationship was not monotonic. The useful output was deliberately small: select
P50 for a frozen forward/paper test, stop percentile testing on the fully viewed panel, and retain
the Pine/configuration/trade evidence properly on the next read.

This qualifies as progress even though it is not admission evidence. It reduced one live design
choice from three settings to one and bought a better next observation. Its limitations—manual
screenshot reads, leg-level rather than day-level win rate, incomplete property capture, fully
viewed history, and informal K—remain attached to its license.

**Common feature:** evidence tier and action tier match. Exploratory evidence may select the next
test; it may not promote capital, claim lifecycle confirmation, or erase multiplicity.

## 3. The advantages the programme has actually discovered

### Advantage A — A strong constraint engine

The estate knows much more than “does this backtest have alpha?” It can ask whether a strategy is
expressible on the venue, clears realistic costs, survives trailing drawdown, respects intraday
barriers, remains stable across regime halves, and improves a portfolio rather than merely looking
good alone. That turns vague ideas into fast, discriminating questions.

**Lean in:** expose these constraints as an intake scorecard. Make every candidate state its gross
effect prior, cost ratio, cadence, hard-stop geometry, minimum-size feasibility, regime claim, and
portfolio role before a bespoke harness is funded.

### Advantage B — Cheap computation and reusable research infrastructure

Agent-hours, repository reads, dry-runs, and many simulations are cheap relative to operator
attention and paid data. Reusing production primitives and frozen parsers also makes follow-up
tests faster and safer than building new analysis stacks.

**Lean in:** spend cheap capacity on exhaustive pre-flight checks, invariant tests, sensitivity
maps, and adversarial reruns—but serialize operator decisions and paid extensions behind those
checks. Reuse an existing engine unless the research question is explicitly about the engine.

### Advantage C — Institutional memory of failure modes

The catalog, rejected candidates, instrument ledgers, preregistrations, corrections, and lessons
make the programme unusually good at detecting disguised repeats: a new label on an old mechanism,
a cost-dead transplant, an invalid proxy, or a regime aggregate hiding a bad half.

**Lean in:** make deduplication causal rather than lexical. Compare mechanism, sign source, holding
window, cost geometry, and prior null mode. A distinguishable mechanism can proceed; a renamed
parameterization should inherit the earlier kill unless it brings specified new evidence.

### Advantage D — Honest negative-result throughput

The programme can terminate weak paths cheaply and document exactly what would justify reopening
them. That is valuable when the search space is large and most ideas should fail.

**Lean in:** measure throughput as **decision-weighted uncertainty retired**, not studies run,
files written, or candidates kept alive. A $0 kill of a doomed paid-data branch is a high-value
result; an elaborate inconclusive model with no routing consequence is not.

### Advantage E — Adversarial self-correction

Independent review, positive controls, mutation tests, both-halves gates, and “what this does not
license” sections have repeatedly caught overclaims and implementation defects.

**Lean in:** reserve the strongest adversarial checks for survivors and decision-near results.
Use a positive control where a method's ability to detect a known effect is in question; use
mutation or fixture tests where an implementation invariant could silently pass vacuously.

### Advantage F — Review can cheaply recover sound efficiency improvements

The PR #258 outcome is not “never optimize a frozen design.” It separates optimizations into two
classes: output-identical implementation changes, which can survive with identity tests, and
precision/design changes, which must be justified against the exact decision they affect and
ratified prospectively. The surviving baseline cache is valuable precisely because bitwise
equivalence is testable; reducing replicates on a gating study was not free merely because the
nominal null remained calibrated.

**Lean in:** review proposed savings in the units the authorization actually consumes. Measure
CPU, wall time, peak memory, checkpoint volume, and dollars separately; make the committed
reproducer compute the proposal actually under review; require identity checks before calling an
optimization semantics-preserving.

## 4. A filter for forward motion versus wheel-spinning

Before starting an action, require a one-screen **decision contract**:

| Field | Required question | Forward-motion test |
|---|---|---|
| Decision | What choice will this evidence change? | At least two possible outcomes route to different actions. |
| Consumer | Who or what uses the answer next? | A live queue item, gate, implementation, or named successor exists. |
| Prior | Why might the effect be large and net positive—or what bounded design choice is being screened? | Admission work needs magnitude evidence; source-stage comparison may only select a frozen next test. |
| Expression | Can the trade be simulated without inventing missing policy? | Signal, entry, fill, stop, exit, sizing, cadence, and session semantics are sufficiently frozen. |
| Reachability | Could success clear real constraints? | Venue, cost, minimum size, power, payoff shape, and data lineage pass a cheap pre-flight. |
| Test | What is the cheapest discriminating observation? | It can falsify or materially reroute the branch; it is not just descriptive. |
| Gate | What numbers mean stop, hold, and proceed? | Frozen before outcome access; marginal/ambiguous is not coerced into pass. |
| Stop | When does the branch end? | A cap on rounds/forks and a re-proposal bar are explicit. |
| Integrity | How could the result be silently wrong? | Canonical inputs, fixtures/controls, provenance, and an orthogonal survivor check are named. |

If **Decision**, **Consumer**, or **Expression** fails, do not begin empirical work. If an admission
study lacks a credible **Prior**, reject it at intake. A bounded source-stage comparison may proceed
without admission-grade prior evidence only when it chooses among already-specified alternatives,
has cheap sunk infrastructure, and licenses no more than a frozen new-data test. If
**Reachability** fails, close before richer inference. If **Gate** or **Stop** is absent, the task is
not yet research-ready.

### Quick portfolio score

For ordering otherwise-ready actions, use:

> **Priority = probability the result changes a live decision × value of that change × evidence
> reliability ÷ (operator attention + cash + irreversible complexity).**

This is an ordering heuristic, not a pseudo-precise numeric model. Its purpose is to expose why a
$0 arithmetic falsifier with a live consumer outranks a fascinating paid dataset with no admitted
candidate.

## 5. Wheel-spinning signatures and default dispositions

| Signature | Why it spins | Default disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Source-liveness or data engineering for a dropped strategy | Proves data exists, not that an edge exists; no consumer remains. | `STOP` until a qualifying candidate creates demand. |
| Broad sourcing without a magnitude prior | Produces mechanism sketches that Vet must finish inventing. | Reject at intake; require credible net-effect evidence and full expression. |
| Paid or high-complexity work before arithmetic/cost/venue screens | Refines an economically unreachable idea. | Run the cheapest reachability kill first. |
| Retuning thresholds, windows, or instruments after a failure | Converts falsification into unbounded researcher degrees of freedom. | Close; reopen only against a pre-stated new-evidence bar. |
| More simulation on an unidentified or invalid signal | Shrinks error bars around the wrong estimand. | Repair identification/proxy validity first. |
| Another model round after the design's own adequacy stop fires | Complexity masks ambiguity rather than resolving it. | Return `AMBIGUOUS-DESIGN` and redesign only if decision value warrants it. |
| Compute “right-sizing” justified only under the null or by an assumed effect location | Can change defect-detection power or verdicts in precisely the alternatives the pilot exists to test. | Preserve gating precision; reduce only non-gating work or prove output identity. |
| Resource estimate uses wall time as CPU time, asserted memory, or a stale reproducer | Makes cost authorization depend on the wrong experiment and wrong capacity unit. | Measure each resource and require the reproducer to emit the reviewed design. |
| A pass that has no licensed next action | Creates a headline without movement. | Rewrite the gate and routing before running. |
| Artifact production, status restatement, or duplicate tracking | Consumes review attention while leaving beliefs and actions unchanged. | Amend the canonical owner or delete the task. |
| Keeping a lane alive because the queue is empty | Optimizes visible activity instead of candidate quality. | Accept `NO CANDIDATE`; improve sourcing inputs. |

## 6. Recommended operating loop

1. **Source fewer, stronger candidates.** Require a credible large net-expectancy prior and a
   complete expression at the door. Search breadth is useful only upstream of this bar.
2. **Run a standard pre-flight.** Dedup → venue/session legality → cost ratio → minimum-size and
   stop geometry → cadence/power → data lineage. Close obvious deaths without a card or custom
   harness.
3. **Write the decision contract.** Freeze the consumer, pass/fail routes, ambiguity route,
   evidence tier, and re-proposal bar.
4. **Explore cheaply and asymmetrically.** A fail kills; a pass purchases only the next evidence
   tier.
5. **Confirm serially.** Use held-out/time-split evidence and then orthogonal realism checks.
   Avoid parallel forks until the previous gate establishes that the branch deserves them.
6. **Adversarially audit decision-near survivors.** Check provenance, look-ahead, window scope,
   cost sensitivity, tail-consistent sizing, intraday honesty, and placebo/positive-control
   behavior as applicable.
7. **Match evidence tier to action tier.** A viewed-panel comparison may choose a frozen forward
   test; it cannot act as confirmation. Record what the result licenses and what it does not.
8. **Route immediately.** Promote, hold with a dated/criterion trigger, or close with a precise
   re-proposal bar. Stop infrastructure and source work when its consumer disappears.
9. **Review the portfolio by decision delta.** Each week ask: which actions changed a live choice,
   eliminated meaningful search space, prevented spend, or corrected a consequential belief?
   Deprioritize everything else.

## 7. Bottom line

The programme should not try to become better at keeping more research alive. It should become
better at **feeding high-prior, fully expressed ideas into the elimination machinery it already
does well**. The winning pattern is:

> **strong prior → complete expression → cheap reachability screen → frozen asymmetric gate →
> orthogonal survivor tests → immediate routing.**

An action moves the programme forward when its plausible outcomes change what happens next. If the
same next action follows whether the result is positive, negative, or unavailable, the action is
almost certainly wheel-spinning.
