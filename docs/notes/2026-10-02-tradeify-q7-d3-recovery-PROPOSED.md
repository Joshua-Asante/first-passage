## What recovery can currently salvage

`execution/worker.py::run_part_a_body` writes the initial Part A prefix through `write_part_a_artifact` (exclusive create, flush, fsync), before expansion, then writes the final artifact after computation. `execution/compute.py::run_part_a_compute` passes the same prefix bytes through custody and its return. This is an existing producer, but its storage is a guardian-bound tmpfs, not durable service-owned custody. The guardian archives the bytes only best-effort on worker nonzero-exit or absent-frame paths. Service-restart/guardian-loss recovery stages no prefix before cleanup. Thus the existing opportunity is a worker-only interruption observed by a surviving guardian whose archive succeeds; service restart or guardian loss retains zero records today and remains terminal under ENG-4.

N1/N2 produce the complete result frame at the end. They do not provide an equivalent progressive complete-record artifact. With zero-record recovery disabled, the demonstrated retained-record opportunity is Part A after its initial prefix, before finalized capture, only on that successfully archived worker-interruption route. Do not broaden that opportunity to arbitrary partial spool bytes or presume a new N1/N2 producer.

`campaign_store.py` currently refuses another compute reservation for a phase and makes interrupted uncaptured work terminal. The spec expressly says every IN_DOUBT remains terminal until the separate recovery slice is accepted. A prefix alone grants no restart authority. The retry would rerun the entire checkpoint from its first panel, not resume after the prefix.
