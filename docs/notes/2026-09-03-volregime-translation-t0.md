## Template A — `VOLREGIME-NB1` (fails T0)

**Proposed bridge (rejected).** The conditioned quantity is unsigned next-bar range. At the close
of the first RTH M15 bar, `bias_volume` is known. A first-touch stop-entry through that bar’s high
or low, with the opposite extreme as the stop and a 1.0R target, was offered as a claim on the next
bar’s excursion, with side generated by which extreme prints first.

**Why it fails (Codex review of PR #281, accepted 2026-09-03).**

1. **No cited convexity prior.** After the first extreme is touched, reaching a target another full
   trigger-bar range away before the opposite extreme is still a first-passage / breakout-continuation
   path claim. “Expectancy is positive if captured movement exceeds costs” restates the definition of
   positive expectancy. L1–L4’s binary fact — next-bar range exceeds its own ToD-matched median —
   does not establish that path geometry.
2. **Opening-slot mismatch.** Admitted L1–L4 is pooled over 135,958 eligible MNQ M15 trigger bars
   ([`L3 RESULTS`](../../lab/analysis/_inbox/volregime_l3_2026-08-31/RESULTS.md)), scored across
   ToD slots. ToD-matched thresholds remove slot seasonality of the *threshold*; they do not show
   the lift on the first RTH bar this sketch selected. The scored population was not preserved, and
   no pre-payoff opening-slot prior exists.
3. **Comparator error in the draft sketch.** MNQ’s live `bias_volume` is `volume >=` its ToD
   trailing median ([`byyear_l4.py`](../../lab/analysis/_inbox/volregime_byyear_l4_2026-08-31/byyear_l4.py),
   [`candidate3_volume_regime.py`](../../lab/analysis/_inbox/mnq_dailygeom_notice_2026-08-29/candidate3_volume_regime.py),
   [`DESIGN.md`](../../lab/analysis/_inbox/volregime_l5_design_2026-08-31/DESIGN.md)). The draft
   wrote strict `>`. Recorded here so it is not reused.

The draft sketch is retained only as the rejected object (MNQ; first RTH M15; `>=` comparator;
OCO first-touch; 1.0R; flat by 10:00 ET). It is **not** a surviving template and is **not** a
founding freeze.
