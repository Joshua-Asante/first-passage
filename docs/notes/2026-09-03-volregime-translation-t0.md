## Template A — `VOLREGIME-NB1` (survives)

**Bridge.** The conditioned quantity is **unsigned next-bar range**. At the close of the first
RTH M15 bar, `bias_volume` is known. A first-touch stop-entry through that bar’s high or low, with
the opposite extreme as the stop and a 1.0R target, is a claim on the next bar’s excursion. Side
is generated by which extreme prints first; no directional forecast is required. Expectancy is
positive if and only if the captured fraction of the elevated-range distribution exceeds
one-sided costs plus the stopped-out loss. If that inequality fails on a frozen development
partition, the template dies as `MARKET-NULL` or `EXPRESSION-FAIL`, not by retuning the
percentile.

**Why this is not “more range means breakout.”** Breakout-as-direction is not the claim. The
claim is first-passage through a known range during the one bar whose unsigned range the
conditioner predicts.
