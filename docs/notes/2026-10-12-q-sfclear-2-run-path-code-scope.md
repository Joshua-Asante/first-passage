### Cross-component constraints to settle before freezing

- **P0 is a named mode/halt contract, not a fabricated protection tuple.** Propose an explicit personal instance/mode strategy admitted independently of the existing Tradeify policy. Its mode remains NORMAL; intraday halt stops risk-adds and requires cancel/flatten confirmation without entering PROTECTED. Preserve the existing Tradeify validation and daily clock. Do not change frozen constants or use a huge trigger as a bypass.
- **Numerical boundary:** the live halt uses rounded drawdown (`personal_drawdown_halt.py:171`); the existing study scorer tests exact integer `20*(peak-trough) >= 3*peak` (`sfgrowth_acceptance.py:270`). The card must name which observed equity sequence and boundary the replay uses and test exact/adjacent thresholds. Do not silently rewrite either contract to manufacture parity. Likewise distinguish account equity from each adapter's paper-equity state; Striker trip dates and Vanguard's paper-equity path need actual producers.
- **Schedule and roll facts:** Claude supplies the freeze-pinned schedule/generator and independently sourced G4 list. B verifies exact buffers before parsing, including retained source-fact references, list completeness and regeneration identity. Missing/changed hashes refuse before adapter construction or outcome access. No roll dates are inferred from a price discontinuity. A denied day remains a zero weekday in scoring, not a removed observation.
- **Layer boundary:** keep the operational replay and lab scorer separate, exchanging validated serialized route artifacts. No `lab↔ops` imports. #782's `lab/research_utils/sfgrowth_margin.py:115` and #783's `lab/discovery/sfgrowth_acceptance.py:305` are reusable scoring components, not an authorized end-to-end personal runner. Do not reuse the old rung-grid/ranking verdict as Q-SFCLEAR-2's fixed-R1 verdict.
- **Trade output:** `replay.py:46` is a session/leg attribution sidecar and `:200` computes adverse lifetime marks. Neither alone establishes the study's complete per-trade MAE/MFE, initial stop, stop-fill residual, add-tranche and paper-equity/trip record. B must define and test the route-output schema against the actual fill lifecycle before E can score it.
- **Fee identities stay separate:** C's dated Tradovate scoring snapshot and B's retained reference-friction identity are both bound. Convert units once; reject missing/duplicate instruments, nonfinite/negative/underflowed amounts and wrong venue/plan/unit tags. A rate lookup or schema change is not an updated fee snapshot.
