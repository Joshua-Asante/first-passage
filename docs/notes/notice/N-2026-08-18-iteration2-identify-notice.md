## §1–§2 — The observations and why they stand out (Notice)

**A. The finder works; the converter has never fired.** The estate has validated true
regularities repeatedly — W-layer weekly structure (three panels, replicated at higher strength
on NQ/MNQ), D-layer bear-FVG reachability (NQ), CL range-state persistence (real at pooled
construction, canon-attributed). **Every one of them is expression-orphaned**: W died at the E1
round-trip decomposition (WSTRUCT), its sub-weekly transfer is falsified (Q-WLEGB-1), the
D-layer's two MNQ expressions died on level-reachability (MNQFVG-1/MNQPOOL-1), and the CL
finding has no host to condition. Meanwhile **zero confirm partitions have ever been read in
program history** — every kill in the record is IS/train-side.
*Baseline:* a generate→evaluate pipeline should occasionally carry a validated regularity
through expression into confirm-stage evidence. *Delta:* conversion success count = 0, ever,
against ≥3 validated regularities and ~30 candidate-shaped attempts in the last window alone.
*Frequency:* this is not one incident — it is the shape of the entire record.

**B. "Supply" conflates two different bottlenecks.** The 2026-08-16 diagnostic named
supply-death-at-admissibility (WHO) as THE operative pair. The window-wide record splits it:
**sourcing-type intakes die at admissibility (~10 in the window: WHO-dry, Clause-N, Req-2,
INTAKE-DRY), but expressed constructs die at explore/train (~15: CIs straddling zero,
net-negative, N-ACT)** — measured deaths outnumber admissibility deaths once something is
actually expressed. Two bottlenecks, two different remedies, previously one word.
*Baseline:* the diagnostic's single-bottleneck framing. *Delta:* the measured death-stage
distribution. *Frequency:* stable across the whole 08-08→08-16 window.

**C. The generous-bar paradox.** The admissibility bar is genuinely generous (+0.10R@$75
clears N-EDGE), yet ~30 attempts produced nothing that survives train. Three candidate
explanations with no discriminating evidence in the record: (a) the tested families are truly
empty (market truth — keep killing); (b) expressions systematically mis-transduce real
regularities (observation A's mechanism); (c) explore designs are underpowered for
modest-but-admissible edges, so true +0.10R-class edges die at the observed rate too. **(a) vs
(c) is a computable discrimination that has never been computed.**
*Baseline:* kills are read as evidence of absence. *Delta:* nobody has checked what kill rate a
true-modest-edge population would produce under the same gates and n's.

**D. The conditioner branch has a typed finding and zero demonstrated economics.** S1b's
SIGNAL-GENERIC is real, canon-attributed, and rails-caged — and the connecting arithmetic from
its measured lift (+0.052 conditional-minus-unconditional, 41st percentile of its own surrogate

the 0.60 anchor is DECLARED-NOT-DERIVED). The branch's continuation is currently justified by
