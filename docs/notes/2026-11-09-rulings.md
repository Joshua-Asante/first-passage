## Rulings

**R1. Scope.** The prop operation is closed. First Passage is strategy research only.
This restores the original consequence of the §4 four-firm falsifier ("demote this program
to research-only", `git show f2c4ae3:docs/adr/2026-07-12-prop-portfolio-four-friendly-firms.md`
lines 78-88), which the 11-03 ruling had narrowed after the outcome was known.

**R2. Research terms.** These are inputs Joshua accepted; under The Order (R10) the round
starts only once he writes and hashes its contract.
- No order, venue, deployment or monetisation work until a Codex-verified CONFIRMED card
  exists. A CONFIRMED card opens only a separate deployment decision (own-account
  economics, legal status, venue, current costs).
- One round. All cards are named together in one registration before any result.
- `round_ceiling = 4` new cards. Each card also records its ancestry and its reconciled
  historical K. MGC and MCL already carry 8 banked cards each, and those still count.
- Allocation: 2-3 index-micro cards, each from a different mechanism family (sparse,
  low-turnover timing effects with a stated mechanism, not ORB parameter variants); at
  most 1 MGC card, only if it was specified before the unread outcomes; 0 cards from the
  cost-killed 6J/M6E breakout families. A slot may stay unused.
- Cards are not chosen because they fit the existing builder.
- The cost re-read at the frozen slip is a kill test. No loosening, replacement, repair,
  neighbour or direction-flip card.
- The round ends at 4 consumed cards or 2026-12-31, whichever comes first. There is no
  automatic second round; another needs new outside evidence, not cheaper search.

**R7. Caps.** Joshua's time is about 1 hour a week; spend is the data line only. These

**R8. Long-horizon thesis and its falsifiers.** Thesis: as search cost falls, trustworthy
evidence about the full search path becomes more valuable, if the evidence capture is
complete, constraining and reproducible, and is shown empirically to improve calibration.
It is falsified by any of these:
- reconciled K does not improve out-of-sample replication over naive search;
- registrations do not constrain independent implementers;
- cross-vendor checking catches no more real defects than same-vendor checking;
- keeping the records costs more than the false positives they prevent;
- candidate generation happens outside the ledger.

**Riskiest assumption** (Codex, adopted): that the recorded K is close to the true search
history, including informal agent proposals and discarded variants. The R2 registration
therefore requires every card's ancestry.

**R9. Prevention measures from Codex's review.** Joshua adopted them for AGENTS.md; The
Order (R10) now carries each one (claim ledger, choose the test, contract caps, the rule
step, weekly review), so AGENTS.md points to it instead of restating them. They are:
- claim IDs that carry fired falsifiers, banked K and reopening conditions into every
  successor (the 07-10 R6 closure was bypassed a week later by a new candidate and
  route);
- a cheapest-falsifier card before any round or build, with only the top-ranked test
  running and cost or reachability failures blocking (the 06-22 cost pre-gate had been
  made report-only on 10-24);
- a work-in-progress limit: one hypothesis, one falsifier, one build item, two open
  decisions;
- one-page decision entries whose default is no new work, with both agents' positions
  verbatim and review capped at one challenge and one correction;
- a weekly review that opens with four numbers (falsifiable results, hypotheses closed,
  Joshua's hours, share of work on the next falsifier), and a stop to everything but
  the cheapest kill test after two weeks without a falsifiable result.

**R10. The Order.** [The Order](../framework/the-order.md) governs programme work, in
witness mode: no holder, so contracts, registrations and its six protected rules cannot
be amended, only replaced going forward. Adopted when Joshua records its hash by
scheduled self-email. Its gates are enforced by the `order-gates` check and the
session-start hook (#1176), so they hold without his remembering them.
