## §2 — What "build for the envelope" means in a brief

1. **Two expressions, always distinguished:** the *research expression* (whatever measures the hypothesis cleanly — may violate E1) and the *deployable expression* (E1-compliant decomposition). A brief may gate on the research expression; it must pre-register the deployable decomposition and annotate `DEPLOYABLE-DEFAULT-ENVELOPE: YES/NO` at closure with a frozen, binary criterion (cost hurdle computed at the deployable expression's round-trip count).
2. **Consistency annotation:** state qualifying-event frequency and P&L-bearing days/yr; if concentrated, mark portfolio-slot-only.
3. **Excursion reporting:** report per-trade excursion stats against E3's intraday-trail posture for any candidate advancing to a deployment fork.
4. **No firm names in research artifacts.** Firm-specific facts enter only at the deployment fork via §4 overlays.
5. **Exposure-coordinates annotation** ([ADR 2026-07-13](../docs/adr/2026-07-13-stage8-mechanistic-exposure-companion.md) §2b): at closure, alongside `DEPLOYABLE-DEFAULT-ENVELOPE`, declare {unconditional or regime-conditional side; entry session window (ET); expected in-market minutes/yr; per-book-leg structural overlap = expected simultaneous-in-market minutes/yr × sign-agreement} — so the exposure-coordinates travel to the deployment fork (consumed by the account-boundary joint MC per [ADR 2026-07-13-prop-account-book-segregation](../docs/adr/2026-07-13-prop-account-book-segregation.md) §2). Declarations describe, never filter.
6. **Risk-breadth + variance-dominance coordinates** ([ADR 2026-07-20](../docs/adr/2026-07-20-stage8-variance-dominance-risk-neff-gate.md) §2; **Superseded-in-part by [`W4 ADR`](../docs/adr/2026-08-07-w4-minimal-gate-set-dormancy.md)** — breadth tool tombstoned as live producer): for any candidate advancing to a deployment fork **as a book leg**, declare — alongside item 5's exposure-coordinates — its `n_eff_risk_delta` and `ρ = candidate daily-$std / existing-book daily-$std` at the deployable weight when a producer is available. **While breadth is tombstoned, these coordinates are report-optional doctrine, not a sole-producer hard gate.** When a producer is re-armed: admissibility as a book leg requires `n_eff_risk_delta > 0`; `ρ ≥ 1.0` is a presumptive reject. Dependence/correlation breadth alone is never the grant (M-21). Never signal-level entry.

## §3 — What the envelope does NOT govern

Research validity (mechanism verdicts, statistical gates, monitors) is envelope-independent — a candidate can be mechanism-RESOLVED and envelope-undeployable; that is a valid, useful outcome (it prices the funded-tier-holds / per-firm-exception forks). The envelope also does not govern Databento scope, K accounting, or any strategy-validation mechanics.

## §4a — Hedging / correlated-products overlay (Tradeify, verified 2026-07-22; re-verified 2026-10-02)

**Why this is an overlay and not yet a default rule:** hedging prohibitions are near-universal at prop firms, so this is a candidate to graduate into §1 as **E8**. It is recorded here first because §1–§3 change only by ADR (see header change control) while §4 overlays change on re-verification. Graduating it requires an ADR that first re-verifies the other three FRIENDLY firms' hedging rules — do not promote it on this single-firm read.

Source: [`help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10495868`](https://help.tradeify.co/en/articles/10495868) ("Rules: Hedging & Correlated Products", article updated 2026-07-21, read 2026-07-22; **re-read 2026-10-02, article dated 2026-08-19** — rule, Product Group table and consequences unchanged; automated-check profit threshold changed, see Detection).

**The rule.** Holding **opposing directions** (one long, one short) on the same instrument *or on two products from the same Product Group* is a violation — **in one account or across any accounts under your control**. Direction and Product Group are the deciding factors; **contract size is irrelevant** (a micro leg against a mini leg is still a hedge).

| Product Group | Symbols |
|---|---|
| **Equity Index** | ES, MES, NQ, **MNQ**, YM, **MYM**, RTY, M2K, EMD, NKD, FDAX, FDXM, FDXS, FESX, FSXE, FXXP |
| Energy | CL, QM, MCL, NG, QG |
| Metals | GC, QO, MGC, SI, HG, PL, PA |
| Currencies | 6E, M6E, 6B, 6J, 6A, M6A, 6C, 6S |
| Interest Rates | FGBL, FGBM, FGBS, FGBX |
| Grains | ZS, ZL, ZM, ZC, ZW |
| Livestock | HE, LE, GF |
| Volatility | FVS |

Mini/micro pairs always share a group. **Volatility is cross-linked to Equity Index**: long Equity Index + *long* FVS is treated as offsetting. The list is explicitly **not exhaustive** — "Tradeify may treat any other position it determines to be offsetting as a hedge."

**Detection.** The *automated* check breaches only when **all three** hold simultaneously: opposing positions exist **AND** hedge duration **> 10 seconds** **AND** hedge profit **> $150** (**CHANGED 2026-10-02** from > $250; quote: "Profit generated from the hedge exceeds $150" [STANDING, all account types]). Load-bearing caveat: those thresholds are **not a safe harbour for the correlated-product case** — the article states "Cross-account hedging and hedging across correlated products are monitored **separately** and reviewed by our risk team," with no published threshold. A same-group, different-symbol hedge (e.g. MYM vs MNQ) falls in that separately-reviewed bucket.

**Consequences.** Disqualification (Evaluation accounts), payout denial, violation status on **every** account involved, forfeiture of profits generated during the violating period, and permanent ban for deliberate or repeated violations.

**Design consequence for this envelope.** Any book whose legs sit in the same Product Group must be **sign-constrained**, not merely decorrelated — the §2 item-5 exposure-coordinates already declare "unconditional or regime-conditional side," and that declaration now carries a compliance meaning, not just a covariance one. A candidate that is long/short-capable on an Equity Index product cannot share an account (or *any* account under the same control) with an opposing-signed Equity Index leg. **Precedence vs §2 item-6 (RESOLVED):** a *negatively*-correlated same-group leg is exactly what `n_eff_risk_delta > 0` rewards and exactly what this rule forbids — compliance screens first, absolutely. Resolved by (1) the c1 GO ADR's 2026-07-22 addendum ([`docs/adr/2026-07-17-c1-rail-build-account-registration-go.md`](../docs/adr/2026-07-17-c1-rail-build-account-registration-go.md) §5 forbidden-move): "Decorrelation candidates must now be screened for **Product Group + sign** before scoring"; and (2) the third-leg target spec's adjudication order ([`docs/spec/2026-07-27-third-leg-target-spec.md`](../docs/spec/2026-07-27-third-leg-target-spec.md) §6.2 / §7.1 S4): `SCREEN-FAIL` on S4 closes the candidate before R4 (risk-breadth) is ever scored. (T4 residual from [`Q-OBJCOHERE-1`](../docs/briefs/closures/Q-OBJCOHERE-1-closure-falsified-coherent.md).)

**Contract-cap corollary (same article).** The cap is **account-aggregate**: "Your combined position must stay within your account's contract limit, counted at 10 micros = 1 mini." A multi-leg book on one account shares one cap. MFFU states the consequence of exceeding it outright — "can result in a **breach** of the trading account" ([13286542](https://help.myfundedfutures.com/en/articles/13286542), verified 2026-07-22) — so treat over-cap as a rule breach, not a rejected order, unless a firm confirms otherwise in writing.
