## Findings

**Measurement groups and like-for-like limits.** These are compatibility judgments from cited methods, not new performance results.

- **EOD native/decompounded books:** rows J (07-11 futures3), A-c05 (H-SOLO), A-J1 (older solo survival), and the original C combined-book readings share a daily-resolution limitation. They differ in **dataset, code binding, cost basis, initial capital, window and sizing construction**.
